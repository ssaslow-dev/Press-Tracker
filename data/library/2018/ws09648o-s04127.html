Community meetings in the San Fernando Valley, Oct. 15

By Holly Andres

The Department of City Planning holds a meeting on Oct. 15 at North Hollywood Regional Branch Library to discuss long-term plans that establish policies and land use in the southeast San Fernando Valley. (Google Street View)

Community meetings bring people together for exchange of ideas and memorable shared experiences plus the potential for positive personal and community enrichment. Here are this week’s, and upcoming, get-togethers.

Southeast Valley Community Plans open houses: The Los Angeles Department of City Planning is beginning a three-year process of formulating long-term plans that establish policies and land use in the southeast San Fernando Valley (North Hollywood, Sherman Oaks, Studio City, Toluca Lake, Valley Village, Van Nuys and the Cahuenga Pass area). Share your opinions at the first meeting, specifically on North Hollywood, Toluca Lake and Valley Village, 5:30-7:30 p.m. Oct. 15. North Hollywood Regional Branch Library, 5211 Tujunga Ave. Upcoming meetings: Discussing Van Nuys and north Sherman Oaks, 5:30 p.m. Oct. 22 (Marvin Braude Constituent Building, room 1A, 6262 Van Nuys Blvd.); check the website for meetings, at different venues, on Oct. 29 and Nov. 7. More information on how to get involved in the planning on the website. www.southeastvalleyplans.org

Taxes, Typhus and Recall Elections: The topic will be discussed at a meeting of Valley Vote, 6:30 p.m. Oct. 15. Los Angeles Daily News columnist Susan Shelley is Valley Vote’s president. Galpin Ford, second floor conference room, 15555 Roscoe Blvd., North Hills. susan@susanshelley.com
