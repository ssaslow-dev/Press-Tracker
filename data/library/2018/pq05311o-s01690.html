Trump’s trade tariffs a bad idea: Letters

Re “Trump announces stiff trade tariffs, unswayed by warnings” (March 8): I am totally against President Trump’s tariff proposals because it will cause the price of many consumer goods to rise. We already have Gov. Jerry Brown’s gas tax doing that. We don’t need another.

— Gregg Harkness, Canyon Country Jesse Gabriel not a good fit for assembly district Re “Assembly candidates for Valley seat debate” (Feb. 21): Jesse Gabriel has a well-managed and well-financed campaign for Assembly District 45. I went to a town hall where all candidates appeared. Gabriel’s closing statement was all about suing the Trump administration. Nothing about what he would do for this district.

This man would not be a good representative. He most likely sees the office as a stepping stone in a political career.

Overwhelming number of flyers sent to my house is not a reason for his election.

— Don Evans, Canoga Park Mayor should focus on job Re “Doug McIntyre: LA mayor learns wrong lesson from ‘Hamilton’ (March 4): Absolutely loved Doug McIntyre’s comments on our mayor. So much has gone wrong in our city under his watch. It just makes me nuts that while our city has all these issues he is out trying to better his chances to become president in the future.

How about doing the job you were elected to do first. And then there’s the pay to play stuff that always seems to get swept under the rug. I will not be voting for Eric Garcetti for president ever. When will we get a mayor that wants to really wants to do the job of running the city.

— Mary Williams, Chatsworth Federal vs. state laws Gov. Jerry Brown’s resistance to federal law reminds me of the state’s rights posture of former Alabama Democrat Gov.

George Wallace.

Remember when he stood at the University of Alabama proclaiming defiance of federal law? He did so to prevent the integration of the University. Yet he succumbed and stepped aside admitting the sovereignty of federal vs. state law.

The Democrats in power in this state remind me of the Dixiecrats who disobeyed federal statutes to prevent integration.

The situation is similar: state vs. federal law. The Democrat governor lost then and will lose now.

— Harry Nieves, Santa Clarita Helping the homeless Re “What happens when homeless are moved to motels?” (Feb. 28): Let’s cross our fingers that the multiple programs to relocate and assist the homeless does not result in yet another debacle.

After countless hours of volunteers working on various programs, taxation strategy, community committees, rules and regulations, the first of the homeless to be relocated claim to be upset by their new accommodations.

Oh, we don’t have a TV, mini-fridge, clock radio — all the amenities are gone! Are they joking? They were moved from a riverbed, pulled out from beneath bushes on the roadside to find themselves in a clean, comfortable room with a bed, heat/cooling and a working bathroom.

If the people now living in a clean, warm government subsidized “home” want to complain, let’s deliver them back into the bushes and give their space to someone appreciative.

— Mindy Tennen, Valley Village

Tax increase proposals Still bristling over the liberals targeting a minority with excessive taxation (tobacco users), I propose the following additional tax increases:

• Since electric autos do not pay gas taxes which fund the roads they use, a $1,000 per year use permit or surcharge to their registration fees; Hybrids $600.

• Increase taxes on beer $1 per container 16 ounces or less, $2 on larger containers.

• $1 on wine containers less than one liter; $2 on containers two liters or less; $5 on containers over two liters.

• $1 on containers of distilled spirits one-half pint or less; $2 on containers of one pint or less; $3 on containers up to onehalf gallon and $5 on any container of distilled spirits larger than one-half gallon. Sorry, liberals, relaxing at the end of the day with a glass of Chardonnay just got more expensive.
