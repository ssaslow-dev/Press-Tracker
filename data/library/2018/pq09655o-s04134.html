ESSENTIAL POLITICS; STATE LEGISLATURE; State Assembly swears in two new Valley members

By Jazmine Ulloa

Democrats Luz Rivas and Jesse Gabriel were sworn in to the Assembly last week after winning special elections to replace two former San Fernando Valley members accused of sexual misconduct.

Rivas, a science educator and Los Angeles Department of Public Works commissioner, will fill the remaining term of former Democratic Assemblyman Raul Bocanegra in the 39th district. Gabriel, an Encino attorney with degrees from UC Berkeley and Harvard, will take over for former state Assemblyman Matt Dababneh, a Democrat, in the 45th district. -- Jazmine Ulloa
