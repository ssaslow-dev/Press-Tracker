San Fernando Valley Assemblyman Jesse Gabriel gets a ‘community’ swearing in

It’s been three months after he was actually elected, but Assemblyman Jesse Gabriel was the focus of a “community swearing-in” on Sunday, as he begins his term representing the people of the 45th District.

Show Caption of Expand The district includes the west Valley communities of Calabasas, Hidden Hills, Canoga Park, Woodland Hills, West Hills, Winnetka, Northridge, Reseda, Tarzana and Encino and the Ventura County community of Bell Canyon.

Gabriel, a constitutional rights attorney — and a Democrat — won a June special election after Matt Dababneh, also Democrat, resigned from the Assembly late last year after being accused by several women of sexual misconduct. That race pitted Gabriel against Justin Clark in a campaign that contrasted Gabriel’s critique of the Trump administration with Clark’s calling out of a culture of corruption among politicians.

Sunday’s event drew several high-profile elected leaders, including L.A. Mayor Eric Garcetti. It also included a service project in partnership with LA Family Housing. Participants pack hundreds of outreach kits with essential hygiene products for people living in homelessness.
