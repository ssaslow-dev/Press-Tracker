Speaker Rendon Announces Assembly Leadership and Committee Assignments

by ECT Dec 28, 2018

written by ECT Dec 28, 2018

2.5K

On Thursday, Assembly Speaker Anthony Rendon announced assignments for the membership of Assembly leadership positions and standing committees for the 2019-2020 legislative session.

Newly elected Assemblymember Rebecca Bauer-Kahan was named to a leadership position as she was named assistant speaker pro tempore. Meanwhile, Assemblyman Jim Frazier maintained Chair of the Transportation committee.

Locally, here is what committee’s local assembly members will serve on:

Rebecca Bauer-Kahan

  • Assistant Speaker pro Tempore
  • Banking & Finance
  • Environment Safety and Toxic Materials
  • Privacy and Consumer Protection
  • Public Safety

Tim Grayson

  • Banking & Finance
  • Business & Professions
  • Insurance
  • Rules
  • Transportation

Jim Frazier:

  • Budget Committee
  • Budget Subcommittee No. 1 on Health and Human Services
  • Insurance
  • Transportation – Chair
  • Veterans Affairs

Buffy Wicks

  • Banking & Finance
  • Budget
  • Budget Subcommittee No. 4 on State Administration
  • Privacy and Consumer Protection
  • Public Safety
  • Rules
