Valley 200 2023: Diana Williams

By staff-author

July 28, 2023

153

    Chief Executive, West Valley-Warner Center Chamber of Commerce

    Williams has served as chief of the West Valley-Warner Center Chamber since 2008. She also is the executive director of the chamber’s Community Benefit Foundation, which established the Valley Asian Cultural Festival last year. Williams is on the economic development and beautification community action teams for City Councilmember Bob Blumenfield; the advisory council for Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel; and the Valley News Group. She serves on the board of the United Chambers of Commerce. She has received the New York Life Women in Business Community Leadership Award and was a recipient last year of the United Chambers’ Inspirational Women of the San Fernando Valley Leadership Award.
