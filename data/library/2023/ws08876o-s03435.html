Ballot Measure Endorsements

Prop 4: Vote YES on Climate Bond
Vote yes to authorize a $10 billion climate bond that includes critical investments in California’s farms, natural lands, clean water, and parks and includes funding to protect against wildfires, sea level rise, flooding and extreme heat.

Prop 3: Vote YES to repeal of Prop 8 same sex marriage ban
Vote yes to enshrine the right to marriage in the California constitution.

Prop 6: Vote YES to end forced prison labor in California
Vote yes to prioritize rehabilitation for incarcerated people and enhance public safety and reduce recidivism by prioritizing rehabilitation, leading to significant cost savings for taxpayers.
