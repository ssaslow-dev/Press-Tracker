CA AB 1305, bill on voluntary offsets disclosure requirements, passes assembly and moves to CA Senate

CCOs / WCOs News

Policy

Friday, 9th June 2023

The Desert Review

Assembly Bill (AB) 1305, authored by Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel (D – Woodland Hills), would impose disclosure requirements for both buyers and sellers of voluntary offset credits in California. The disclosure requirements include: regsitry of project, project name, number, methodology and location among other details, as well as how entities claiming carbon neutrality are calculating their emissions balance. The bill passed through the CA assembly and will now be considered by the Senate.
