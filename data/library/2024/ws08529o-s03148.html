Take a Deep Breath: We Can Defeat Trump

The breaking news from a (partisan) special counsel, followed by a news conference from Biden, was…challenging.

On top of it all, economic “vibes” are weird. A noticeable increase in Black and Latino men are slowly joining their white peers in supporting Trump. There’s the fear that Gen Z, Arab, and other voters will hold off from voting or volunteering for Biden because of the Israel-Hamas war.

I need you to take a deep breath and say the old mantra: Yes We Can.

The Democratic Party is in a better position now than it has been for generations, rooted in the anti-MAGA coalition’s performance in 2018, 2020, 2022, and 2023.

Nothing is pre-ordained, but pessimism and nihilism only fuels self-fulfilling prophecy. It’s also a cop-out from doing the work needed to win.

Biden’s age is the media’s new “but her emails,” and we have to fight back by doing, not worrying.

Yair Rosenberg offers insights in What Biden’s Critics Get Wrong About His Gaffes:

Even a cursory history of Biden’s bungling shows that he is the same person he has always been, just older and slower—a gaffe-prone, middling public speaker with above-average emotional intelligence and an instinct for legislative horse-trading. This is why Biden’s signature moments as a politician have been not set-piece speeches, but off-the-cuff encounters….And it’s why Biden’s key accomplishments—such as the landmark climate-change provisions of the Inflation Reduction Act, the country’s first gun-control bill in decades, and the expected expansion of the child tax credit—have come through Congress. The president’s strength is not orating, but legislating; not inspiring a crowd, but connecting with individuals. (emphasis added)

I agree with former GOP operative Stuart Stevens, who is right in his column, Just Say It, Democrats: Biden Has Been a Great President. Biden has a really great track record, despite the noise.

Instead of wallowing in despair, make a plan. Stop worrying, start doing!

Use Oath by Brian Derrick, which will drive your money to Democratic campaigns that need it most. Volunteer with Swing Left, Grassroots Dems HQ, or Indivisible.

Yes, we can win this thing.
