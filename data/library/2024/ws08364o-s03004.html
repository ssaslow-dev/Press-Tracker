California bill could ban certain additives in school meals

by Devin Herenda

Thu, May 30, 2024 at 6:15 PM

Updated Thu, May 30, 2024 at 10:05 PM

School meals are pictured at Mistletoe Elementary School in Redding on Thursday, May 30, 2024. (KRCR)

NORTHSTATE, Calif. — A state bill could change the menus at schools throughout California.

AB 2316 would prohibit public schools from providing students with foods that have certain substances. It would ban food containing titanium dioxide, as well as six food dyes.

In certain circumstances, public schools could still allow those foods to be sold at school fundraising events.

Enterprise Elementary School District Communications Coordinator Matthew Baker told us their staff aims to educate kids in all areas, including nutrition.

Baker said, at EESD schools, their food team tries to make sure the major food groups are met on their menus, including all their fruits and vegetables being sourced locally.

“The whole point of schools and education is to set kids up to make great decisions in life, right? So, whether that be what job they’re going to have later, have the skills to be able to do that – that’s one avenue. Another thing is, if we truly want healthy kids that thrive in life, we have to teach them to make good choices now,” Baker said. “Being able to do that through literally the plate of food that they have in front of them at lunchtime – that helps our kids to be successful as they go on in life.”

Mistletoe Elementary School students eat in their school cafeteria, Thursday, May 30, 2024. (KRCR)

Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel, a co-author of the bill, said AB 2316 "will empower schools to better protect the health and wellbeing of our kids and encourage manufacturers to stop using these dangerous additives.”

Baker detailed that, if the bill did become law, the school district would be on top of it, and their menu would change accordingly.

He added, “We want to help kids make healthy choices, and we want to set kids up for the best they can be in life. And food is absolutely one part of that.”
