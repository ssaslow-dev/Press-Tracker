Serving Calabasas Since 1963 November 7, 2024 Local Election Results The Rotary Club of Woodland Hills is holding their 8th Annual Texas Hold ‘Em Poker and Casino Night fundraiser on Sunday, November 17. Doors open at 2 pm and the tournament starts at 2:30 pm at Calabasas Community Center, 27040 Malibu Hills Road. Tickets are $150 for a poker ticket; $110 for casino tickets. The evening includes table games, a poker tournament, and prizes. Attendance includes dinner, one alcoholic beverage and unlimited soft drinks. Part of the proceeds will benefit Rotary’s programs for veterans and seniors. The event will honor Rotary District 5280 Governor Cozette Vergari and her passion toward stamping out human trafficking. For tickets visit rotarycasinonite.com. Fairwinds Hosts Veterans Day Celebration Fairwinds West Hills will host a Veterans Day celebration to honor all those who served on Sunday, November 10, from 11 am to 2 pm. Join them for a BBQ, signature drinks and raffle prizes. Register to win a $500 gift card. An opening salute and color guard will take place at 11 am, with 11:30 am lunch and 12:30 pm music and dancing. RSVP to (818) 713-0900. Jesse Gabriel Re-Elected Congressman Brad Sherman (D) was re-elected as United States Representative for the 32nd District, which includes most of the San Fernando Valley. He received 65.02% of the votes with challenger Larry Thompson (R) receiving 34.98%. Nathan Hochman, (N) a former federal prosecutor, U.S. Assistant Attorney General, and LA Ethics Commission President, received 61.35% of the vote to become L.A.’s new District Attorney, over incumbent George Gascon (N) who received 38.65%. California Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel (D) held on to his seat in the 46th District, which covers from Bell Canyon through Encino and parts of Van Nuys, with 6l.0% of the vote with challenger Tracey Schroeder (R) receiving 39.0%. Julia Brownley Re-Elected. Henry Stern Re-Elected Jacqui Irwin Re-Elected Julia Brownley (D) was re-elected as United States Representative for the 26th District, which runs from Ventura through Thousand Oaks into Calabasas. She won with 55.8% of the votes with challenger Michael Koslow receiving 44.2%. State Senator Henry Stern, (D) whose 27th District covers from Agoura to Lake Balboa and from Woodland Hills up to Simi Valley, was re-elected to his seat with 63.60% of the vote, with challenger Lucie Volotzky (R) receiving 36.40%. California Assemblymember Jacqui Irwin (D), held on to her seat in the 42nd District, which includes Agoura, Calabasas, Hidden Hills, Topanga and Malibu, with 59.47% of the vote with challenger Ted Nordblum (R) receiving 40.53%. Rotary and Sagebrush To Serve Seniors Thanksgiving Dinner The Rotary Club of Calabasas will host their 34th annual Thanksgiving luncheon for Seniors on Thursday, November 28. The complimentary holiday meal will be prepared by the Sagebrush Cantina staff and served by volunteers from all over the community, including Rotary club members. Turkeys have been donated by various Rotary clubs, including Woodland Hills. St. Bernardine Church donates all the pies, and the preparation and labor is donated by the Sagebrush Cantina team, under the direction of owner Donal Tavey and manager Mario Romero. Seniors from the community, assisted living and retirement centers are invited as guests. Doors open at 11 am with lunch served at 11:30 am.
