SACRAMENTO — On Friday, April 12, Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel (D-Encino) applauded the announcement that the State of California has awarded $40 million in security grants to nonprofits facing hate motivated violence — including over $6 million awarded to nonprofits serving residents of Assembly District 46 in the San Fernando Valley.

According to a press release from the Office of Jesse Gabriel, in total, 29 nonprofits that serve Assembly District 46 residents were awarded $6.1 million for critical security enhancements to their facilities. Nearly $3 million in funding was allocated to nonprofits located within Assembly District 46, including the Holy Martyrs Armenian Apostolic Church in Encino, the AGBU Manoogian-Demirdjian School in Canoga Park, Valley Beth Shalom in Encino, Valley Presbyterian Hospital in Van Nuys, Crespi Carmelite High School in Encino, De Toledo High School in West Hills, and Chabad of the Valley in Tarzana.

“I’m grateful that the State of California is providing critical security funding to so many worthy nonprofits across the San Fernando Valley,” said Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel (D-Encino), who serves as Co-Chair of the Legislative Jewish Caucus and who authored the legislation to create the NSGP. “California will not stand idly by as hate and antisemitism continue to rise and while our vulnerable communities are threatened by extremists. This security funding will support critical security enhancements at institutions most in need and will make it clear that California stands with all communities targeted by hate.”
