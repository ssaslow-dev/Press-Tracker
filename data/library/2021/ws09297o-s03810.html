SoCal officials warn of street racing, takeover dangers for Halloween weekend

by: Sandra Mitchell, KTLA 5 Digital Staff, Gil Levyas

Posted: Oct 29, 2021 / 01:06 PM PDT

Updated: Oct 29, 2021 / 10:36 PM PDT

Illegal street racing and street takeovers have become a growing problem in Southern California. On Friday, local leaders gathered outside a AAA office in Northridge to issue a new warning for Halloween weekend.

“Valley residents are fed up with illegal sideshows, they are fed up with street racing, they are fed up with these takeovers that are putting our kids at risk,” said Assemblyman Jesse Gabriel.

Sandra Mitchell, Pedro Rivera and Gil Leyvas report for the KTLA 5 News on Oct. 29, 2021.
