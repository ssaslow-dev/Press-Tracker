Photos: Ex-Rep. Gabrielle Giffords unveils memorial to victims of gun violence at Exposition Park

In 2020, 3,400 people were killed by gun violence in California.

A memorial to those victims opened in Los Angeles’ Exposition Park on Tuesday, Oct. 19. The art installation is part of a national crusade by ex-Rep. Gabrielle Giffords, D-Ariz., to install memorials to remember lives lost to violence and to push for stricter gun laws.

The display features 3,400 vases. A similar memorial was installed in Washington D.C. earlier this year, including 40,000 vases to commemorate lives lost to gun violence nationwide.

The memorial in Los Angeles will be on display until Friday.

A 2011 mass shooting at a Giffords public gathering event outside a Tucson supermarket left six people dead and 13 injured, including the former congresswoman. Shooter Jared Loughner was sentenced to life in prison.
