New bill could ban the sale of Glocks, including the one Kamala Harris owns

If the bill passes through the Legislature and is signed by Gov. Gavin Newsom, anyone looking to purchase a gun in California like the one Kamala Harris owns would be out of luck.

byDaniel Gligich

July 29, 2025

Californians may no longer be allowed to purchase the handgun that former Vice President Kamala Harris owns.

The California Legislature is considering a bill that will ban the sale of new Glock-brand handguns, as well as other brands which offer similar handguns.

The big picture: Assembly Bill 1127 would ban those handguns from being sold because they can become fully automatic with a converter, known as a Glock switch.

  • Violators would be hit with a $1,000 fine, and multiple violations could result in their licenses being revoked.
  • The bill would not affect police officers, who often use Glocks.
  • Asm. Jesse Gabriel (D–Encino) authored the bill.

What we’re watching: The bill passed through the Assembly and is now being considered in the Senate.

  • If AB 1127 becomes law, current Glock owners, such as Harris, would not be affected. They, and everyone else, would simply be unable to purchase another Glock in California.

Flashback: During her presidential run last year, Harris appeared on 60 minutes and told the program that she owned a Glock and had fired it.

  • “I have a Glock, and I’ve had it for quite some time,” Harris told 60 Minutes journalist Bill Whitaker. “Look Bill, my background is in law enforcement, so there you go.”
  • When asked if she ever fired it, Harris responded while laughing, “Yes, of course I have – at a shooting range. Yes, of course I have.”
