AJU welcomes state leader for in-depth look at antisemitism - Beverly Press & Park Labrea News

Edwin Folven

Assemblyman Jesse Gabriel will join AJU president Dr. Jefferey Herbst for the discussion. (photo courtesy of
Assemblyman Jesse Gabriel’s office)

American Jewish University is holding a virtual program on the “State of Antisemitism in California” with Assemblyman Jesse Gabriel (D-Woodland Hills) on Thursday, Jan. 14 at noon. Gabriel will be joined in conversation by AJU president Dr. Jefferey Herbst.

The discussion will focus on the rise of antisemitism in California public universities, ethnic studies curriculums and the safety of Jewish communities. Gabriel and Herbst will shed light on the root causes of antisemitism and explain legislative efforts to combat Jewish hate. A Q&A session follows the event.

Gabriel serves as co-chair of the Legislative Jewish Caucus and chair of the Assembly Budget Committee. The program is part of AJU’s President’s Speaker Series. For information and to register, visit open.aju.edu/event/the-state-of-antisemitism-in-california.
