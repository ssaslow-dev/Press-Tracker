Domestic violence help and hotlines in Pasadena

October is National Domestic Violence Awareness Month, and the Pasadena Police Department Special Victims Unit reminds residents that no one should face abuse alone and that confidential help is available. Support resources include Peace Over Violence — West San Gabriel Valley at 626-793-3385, the National Domestic Violence Hotline (24/7) at 800-799-7233, text START to 88788, online help at thehotline.org, the Pasadena Police Department Non-Emergency line at 626-744-4241, and 911 for anyone in immediate danger.
