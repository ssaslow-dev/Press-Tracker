Antonio Villaraigosa says he’s ‘unleashed’

California Playbook

[Excerpt saved: the newsletter's lead item and the REDISTRICTING WARS item that quotes Gabriel. The full California Playbook also covers the governor's race, House races, the L.A. mayor's race and other items.]

STILL SWINGING — Antonio Villaraigosa decided it was time to approach the governor’s race like a “street fight.” Don’t expect him to stop now.

The former Los Angeles mayor’s punchy performances in last week’s debates drew him arguably the best press of his campaign so far. Unlike in the staid forums where candidates weren’t allowed to call each other out, or the first debates when he abided by restrictive time limits, Villaraigosa told Playbook he made the calculated choice to let it rip.

“I felt unleashed because I felt constrained by the rules,” Villaraigosa told Playbook. “Once I realized there’s no freaking rules, I was gonna start swinging.”

REDISTRICTING WARS — ‘RIGGED THE SYSTEM’ — Republicans just won the redistricting war, Andrew Howard writes, after the Virginia Supreme Court struck down a voter-approved Democratic gerrymander of four seats on Friday. And in California, Democrats are fuming.

“No vote in Tennessee (+1 GOP) No vote in Florida (+4 GOP) No vote in Missouri (+1 GOP) No vote in North Carolina (+2 GOP) No vote in Texas (+5 GOP) Virginia’s voter-approved maps thrown out. MAGA has rigged the system,” Gavin Newsom said in an X post.

Assembly Speaker Robert Rivas, who also participated in California’s redistricting effort, said the decision “erased the votes of more than a million Americans.” “Democrats have shown we can fight back and win, just like we did in California,” Rivas said. “But Trump and Republicans are rigging the map nationwide, and every Democrat in every state needs to answer the call and act.”

Los Angeles-area state Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel called the Virginia ruling “devastating, particularly after the people of Virginia have already weighed in on the matter through the democratic process.” — Lindsey Holden
