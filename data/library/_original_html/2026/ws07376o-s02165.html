Councilmember Blumenfield and LA Cleanteach Incubator launched a community EV shuttle in Warner Center

Staff report

Los Angeles City Councilmember Bob Blumenfield, local elected officials, Los Angeles Cleanteach Incubator and the Warner Center Association recently launched a community electric vehicle shuttle in Warner Center. The shuttles are provided by Circuit, an LACI portfolio company and a leading provider of electric, on-demand, microtransit services.

The on-demand ride service operates anywhere within Warner Center’s boundaries from 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. Monday through Friday and 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Saturdays and Sundays. You can catch a ride for only $1.

The pilot program was created as a partnership between Blumenfield and LACI to make public transportation options more widely available in the West Valley. Blumenfield said, “As Warner Center has seen the largest growth in Los Angeles with residents, businesses, and visitors, this circulator is an exciting addition—making it easier, more affordable, and environmentally friendly for people to get where they need to go.”

Photo caption: Kim Brewer from Westfield, President and CEO of LACI Matt Petersen, Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel, Councilmember Bob Blumenfield, and Brad Rosenheim, Executive Director, Rosenheim & Associates, Inc. at the ribbon cutting of the new EV shuttle service for Warner Center. (Courtesy Councilmember Bob Blumenfield)

Matt Petersen, President and CEO of LACI said, “By launching this EV shuttle pilot here at Warner Center–a far reaching and bustling live-work community in the heart of the West San Fernando Valley–we have an opportunity to expand mobility access for the local residents, commuters, and visitors, while also showcasing the power of zero emissions solutions.”

Daniel Kramer, Vice President of Business Development (West) at Circuit, said, “Warner Center is exactly the kind of community where microtransit can make a real difference, and we’re proud to finally be up and running for the people who live and work here.”
