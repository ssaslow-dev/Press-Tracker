“Too-Long-Didn’t-Read” (TLDR) Voter Guide

WHY WE DO THIS GUIDE:

We‘ve created these voter guides since Trump’s first election because we realized that filling out our ballots from top to bottom wasn’t as easy as we thought. Democratic groups sometimes leave big-ticket offices off their voter slates because their membership couldn’t reach a 60% consensus. Many small and non-partisan office don’t make the cut either. So, we decided to make our own “slate” for every election. Here are some other lessons we’ve learned since 2018:

  • There are no unimportant offices: Various right-wing organizations encourage their people to run for city councils and school boards, to get name recognition to use as a launching pad for higher offices. Indivisible Conejo had to spend part of Trump’s first term ridding their school board of these people. So we started our guide to not only to research headliner candidates and propositions, but also to fill in the neglected back corners of our ballots, like judges and water boards.
  • We are not telling you how to vote! We are telling you how WE would vote based on our research (which is ongoing). Disagree with a selection? Our guide is stuffed with information to help you make your own decisions – each office in the guide below has a link to additional pages.
    • “ENDORSED” – These are our favorites and/or strongest choices
    • “BEST PICK” – Sometimes there are no clear frontrunners, or there may be only GOP candidates involved. In that case, we picked the candidate with the most experience and/or respect from the people we know and trust.
  • Message to candidates: Sweet jeezus, you are running for a public office, not putting your name in a lottery bowl. Yes, we need to know who you are, and who else besides your mom thinks you’re capable of doing the job!
  • There are no perfect candidates!: Non-voters were the largest group in the last presidential election and whatever they were trying to communicate by their non-participation, what we ended up with was a Christo-fascist kleptocracy. So this time, we urge everyone to vote for the best IMPERFECT option to protect our environment, our schools, our civil rights and the people who’ve been endangered. This is the bare minimum necessary to keep our democracy running for when a truly inspirational voice comes to the fore. We can no longer assume that it will without us doing the work.
    Whoever gets elected – then we hold them accountable. Put their contact information in your phone.

Beware of all those glossy slate mailers telling you how to vote!

(calmatters) “There’s nothing illegal about selling endorsement space on a postcard. California law requires that mailers include a “notice to voters,” specifying that the mailer group is “NOT AN OFFICIAL POLITICAL PARTY” (yes, in all caps).

  • Another required disclosure: Candidates and causes that paid for their mailer spot must be listed with an asterisk.
  • What isn’t required: revealing the funders behind the mailer “committee.”
