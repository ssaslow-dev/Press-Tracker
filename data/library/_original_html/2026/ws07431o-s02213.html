Schools are the largest restaurant chain in America, bigger than McDonald's, Subway, and Starbucks combined. They serve 7 billion meals a year to 30 million kids. And most of it is ultra-processed, chemically addictive, and banned in other countries.

This week on the Commune Podcast, I sit down with California Assembly Member Jesse Gabriel and Nora LaTorre, CEO of Eat Real, to talk about what's really on your kid's lunch tray and what it's doing to their health. Doctors are now seeing adult diseases manifest in children. Kids today may live shorter lives than their parents. That's not a talking point. That's a wake-up call.

Jesse and Nora are fighting to change this at the policy level with the Real Food Healthy Kids Act, because when the state serves your child lunch, it becomes the guardian. And right now, we're not living up to that responsibility.

This is an all-hands-on-deck moment.
