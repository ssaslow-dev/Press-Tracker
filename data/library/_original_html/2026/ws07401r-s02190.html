(FOX40.COM) — On Thursday, more than 700 people gathered at the California State Capitol to celebrate Nowruz, the Persian New Year, with a traditional display and community event hosted by MTO Shahmaghsoudi centers in Sacramento and Los Angeles.

The celebration featured a Haft-seen table, a symbolic arrangement central to Nowruz traditions, representing themes such as life, health, love, patience and renewal. Attendees also received handmade bookmarks and roses as part of the festivities.

State Assemblymembers Joshua Hoover and Jesse Gabriel joined the event, helping to recognize the cultural and historical significance of the holiday.

Nowruz, which coincides with the spring equinox, marks the start of the Persian New Year and has been observed for more than 3,000 years across diverse cultures and regions worldwide. The 13-day festival emphasizes renewal, nature and community.

In 2010, Nowruz was added to UNESCO's List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, recognizing its global cultural importance, according to officials.

MTO Shahmaghsoudi, also known as the School of Islamic Sufism, is an international nonprofit organization with a 1,400-year lineage of Sufi teachers. The organization serves a global community of more than one million students, with millions more participating in online programs.

Organizers said in a press release that the event aimed to share the message of Nowruz — renewal, unity and cultural connection — with the broader community.
