Assembly Budget Chair Gabriel Responds to Latest Revenue Forecast

For immediate release:
Wednesday, November 19, 2025
Noah Marty
Legislative/Communications Assistant
Noah.Marty@asm.ca.gov

SACRAMENTO, CA — Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel (D-Encino), Chair of the Assembly Budget Committee, released the following statement regarding the Legislative Analyst’s Office’s new fiscal outlook:

"Today's fiscal outlook underscores the challenging decisions ahead. While the Trump Administration continues to pursue destructive policies that will harm California families, the Assembly Budget Committee remains committed to crafting a responsible budget that prioritizes essential services, uplifts working families, and protects our most vulnerable communities."
