Assemblymember Gabriel Holds 2nd Annual Valley Day of Service

Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel held his second Annual Valley Day of Service on Sunday, April 30 at Pierce College in Woodland Hills.

Mark Nero, Patch Staff

Posted Fri, May 5, 2023 at 2:52 pm PT

SAN FERNANDO VALLEY -- Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel held his second Annual Valley Day of Service on Sunday, April 30 at Pierce College in Woodland Hills.

The West Valley-Warner Center Chamber of Commerce, along with other community groups and nonprofits, partnered with Assemblymember Gabriel to work on service projects around the San Fernando Valley.

Projects included cleaning local neighborhoods, packing homeless outreach kits, a blood drive, supporting seniors and healthcare workers and more.
