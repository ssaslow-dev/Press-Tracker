My 2024 National General Election Recommendations

October 17 2024

Written by Dennis P. Zine.

RANTZ & RAVEZ -If you are undecided and looking for Election Ballot recommendations, I have researched the various Candidates, Offices and Propositions. I believe my recommendations will benefit the people of Los Angeles County. I encourage you to VOTE as soon possible and not wait until the last minute to turn in your Ballot.

The Recommendations are not based on any Political Party; but the ability to deliver Responsible and Effective Legislation, Leadership and Services for ALL Los Angeles City & County Residents, Business Owners and Visitors.
