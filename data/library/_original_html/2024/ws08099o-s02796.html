California pledged $500 million to help tenants preserve affordable housing. They didn't get a dime.

A state program aimed at preserving affordable housing in California has been scrapped after failing to distribute any of the $500 million allocated for the program, leaving tenants and community land trusts unsure if they will be able to keep rents low.
