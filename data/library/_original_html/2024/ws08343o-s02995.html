Gavin Newsom and top Democrats are deciding California's budget behind closed doors

California legislative leaders have passed a placeholder budget ahead of a mandatory deadline, with the state facing a projected multibillion-dollar deficit, and are still negotiating with Governor Gavin Newsom on how to close the gap.
