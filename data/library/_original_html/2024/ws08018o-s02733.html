Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel will host a Holiday Toy Drive on Wednesday, December 11, from 4 to 6 pm to benefit the West Valley Food Pantry. Please bring donations of new, unwrapped toys only. Toys will be distributed at a later date to families in need. The event will be held at ONEgeneration, 17400 Victory Blvd. in Lake Balboa. Register at big.ly/toydrive24. For more information please reach out to Catherine.Coddington@asm.ca.gov or Jack.Trent-Dorfman@asm.ca.gov.
