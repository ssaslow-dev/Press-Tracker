Elections 2020: In Assembly District 45, Gabriel leads Girgenti

Staff report

Incumbent Democrat Jesse Gabriel, who twice sued the Trump administration over DACA, led Republican Jeffi Girgenti in early returns on Tuesday, Nov. 3 in the race for the 45th Assembly seat.

Democrats have won the Assembly District 45 seat for the past decade. Gabriel is running for his second term.

The District: District 45 encompasses about 467,766 people spread throughout L.A. and Ventura counties, including Calabasas, Hidden Hills, Canoga Park, Encino, Northridge, Reseda, West Hills, Winnetka, Woodland Hills, Tarzana, and Bell Canyon.

The Incumbent: Gabriel, who twice sued the Trump administration over DACA, is now running for his second term in District 45. While a Democrat, he’s certainly not a progressive.

The Challenger: Girgenti, a small business owner, received over 900 write-in votes in the primary, and will be allowed to face Gabriel in the general due to the top two-system. She wants to repeal AB5, increase penalties for people with substance abuse disorders, and supports charter schools.
