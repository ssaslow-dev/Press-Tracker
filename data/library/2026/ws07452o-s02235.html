Assembly Budget Chair Jesse Gabriel discusses proposed state budget

In Focus Staff

On this week’s “In Focus SoCal,” host Tanya McRae sits down with Assemblyman Jesse Gabriel, chair of the Assembly Budget Committee.

Gabriel reacts to the governor’s proposed budget for the next fiscal year.

The $349 billion budget does not include new funding for programs supporting unhoused Californians, with the governor touring a 9% drop in unsheltered homelessness in his last State of the State address.
