Disaster Prevention

AB 2102 (DeMaio) Firebreaks and fuel reduction. Status 5/10: Did not make it out of committee.

SB 1270 (Richardson) More funding for home hardening. Status 5/10: Senate Appropriations.

Insurance

AB 1559 (Calderon) Notice required for aerial photos for insurance,
bans cancellations based on 6+ month old images. Status 5/10: Assembly Floor

AB 1680 (Calderon) Major reform of FAIR plan — more options, strategic
planning, climate risk assessment, etc. Status 5/10: Assembly Appropriations.

AB 1986 (Bennett) Requires insurers to provide quotes of what the
cost of insurance would be after hardening. Status 5/10: Did not make it out of committee.

AB 2724 (Bauer-Kahan) Dept of Insurance to review high risk areas having hard time finding insurance and offer suggestions. Status 5/10: Assembly Floor.

SB 876 (Padilla) Faster payout of claims after disasters. Status 5/10: Senate Appropriations.

SB 878 (Perez) More accountability & data on insurance business. Status 5/10: Senate Appropriations.

SB 1076 (Perez) Requires insurers to cover hardened homes. Status 5/10: Did not make it out of committee.

SB 1162 (Alvarado-Gil) Dept of Forestry to work on risk reduction in areas
with high use of FAIR plan. Status 5/10: Senate Appropriations.

SB 1301 (Allen) More notice required for non-renewals. Status 5/10: Senate Appropriations.

Rebuilding and Relief

AB 1857 (Aguiar-Curry) Extends mortgage forbearance for 2025 wildfires. Status 5/10: Assembly Appropriations.

SB 1053 (Niello) Extends deadline to transfer old property tax
assessments for reconstructed property. Status 5/10: Senate Appropriations.

SB 1090 (Perez) Bans people and businesses that own over 75 houses
from buying houses in wildfire disaster area. Status 5/10: Senate Floor.

SB 1352 (Valladares) Property tax relief for reconstructed property. Status 5/10: Senate Appropriations.

Planning

AB 2295 (Johnson) Allows local government to fund affordable housing in another jurisdiction and count portion of units toward RHNA housing production goals. Status 5/10: Did not make it out of committee.

AB 2296 (Papan) Extends the duration of the RHNA process by 6 months, and staggers due dates for cities in each region. Status 5/10: Assembly Appropriations.

AB 2741 (Muratsuchi) Allows affordable housing overlays of nonresidential and mixed use zones to count towards RHNA goals. Status 5/10: Did not make it out of committee.

SB 1361 (Durazo) Bans local government from limiting existing or planned transit to avoid SB79 applicability, originally would have scaled back SB79. Status 5/10: Senate Floor.

Coastal Housing Production

SB 963 (Laird) More certainty of timelines for coastal permits. Status 5/10: Passed Senate 36–0, at Assembly.

Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs)

SB 1117 (Cervantes) Reduces ADU development impact fees- no fees on the first 750 square feet of areas. Status 5/10: Senate Floor.

SB 1196 (McNerney) Faster electrical connections for ADUs. Status 5/10: Senate Appropriations.

Cost Reduction

AB 1406 (Ward) Allows builders to use more of the condo deposit (up to 6% of price, up from 3%) for development costs. Passed Assembly 41–15, in Senate.

AB 1815 (Wicks) Standardizes building codes for factory-built housing. Status 5/10: Assembly Appropriations.

AB 1903 (Wicks) Condo Construction Defect Liability reform. Status 5/10: Assembly Appropriations.

AB 1926 (Pacheco) Requires HCD to review impact of new regulations on housing costs, and also recommend cost reductions. Status 5/10: Assembly Appropriations.

AB 2044 (Petrie-Norris) Requires cost estimate of impact for proposed regulations. Status 5/10: Assembly Floor.

AB 2058 (Harabedian) Allows 3rd-party inspections of factory built housing. Status 5/10: Assembly Appropriations.

AB 2166 (Carrillo) Low cost financing for factory built housing. Status 5/10: Assembly Appropriations.

AB 2252 (Lee) Allows 6-story single stair buildings. Status: Did not make it out of committee.

SB 1036 (Grayson) Limits fees for demolitions, water, sewer, etc. Status 5/10: Passed Senate 37–0, at Assembly.

SB 1415 (Arreguin) Funded inclusionary housing: property tax exemption for low & moderate income units in mixed income building. Status 5/10: Senate Appropriations.

Faster Approvals

AB 1294 (Haney) Statewide standardized planning application. Status 5/10: Passed Assembly 78–0, in Senate committee.

AB 1621 (Wilson) Speeds up building permit review. Status 5/10: Passed Assembly 67–0, in Senate committee.

AB 1710 (Carrillo) Locks in regulations at time of application. Status 5/10: Assembly Appropriations.

AB 1751 (Quirk-Silva) CEQA exemption for townhouses except in San Francisco. Status 5/10: Assembly Appropriations.

AB 1997 (Lee) Faster approvals and more certainty in the process. Status 5/10: Assembly Appropriations.

AB 2118 (Hoover) Cleanup bill for housing in commercial zones (AB2011). Prohibits cities from limiting non-housing uses in a mixed use AB2011 project. Status 5/10: Assembly Floor.

AB 2390 (Schiavo) Cleanup bill for streamlined approvals (SB35/SB423). Status 5/10: Assembly Floor.

AB 2433 (Alvarez) More flexibility & faster approvals for Density Bonus bldgs. Status 5/10: Assembly Appropriations.

AB 2601 (Lee) Allows parcel map splits, ADU, condo, and SB9 splits to proceed in parallel. Allows SB9 condos so all 4 homes can be sold separately. Status 5/10: Passed Assembly 46–14, in Senate.

SB 1014 (Grayson) Requires local govt to provide list of required onsite and offsite improvements for new housing promptly. Status 5/10: Senate Appropriations.

SB 1258 (Wiener) Streamlines cleanup & redevelopment of brownfields. Status 5/10: Senate Appropriations.

SB 1344 (Cabaldon) Doubles potential liability for a plaintiff suing to stop low or moderate income housing, from 500k to 1 million. Status 5/10: Senate Floor.

Other Production Bills

AB 2005 (Ahrens) Allows investors to do SB9 lot splits as long as the lots are sold to owner-occupants. Status 5/10: Assembly Floor.

AB 2074 (Haney) Cities over 400,000 people required to have at least one downtown highrise zone with streamlined approvals. Includes labor standards. Status 5/10: Assembly Appropriations.

AB 2252 (Lee) Single stair — 6 stories. Status 5/10: Did not make it out of committee.

AB 2676 (Gallagher) Strengthens 2019’s SB330, extends ban on housing
moratoriums to sphere of influence around a city. Status 5/10: Assembly Floor.

SB 677 (Wiener) Cleanup bill for housing near transit (SB79). Status 5/10: Passed Senate last year 24–10, in Assembly.

SB 1116 (Caballero) Cleanup bill for small lot subdivisions (SB684/SB1123). Status 5/10: Senate Appropriations.

SB 1169 (Grayson) Extends tentative subdivision expiration date so that slow moving projects don’t expire. Status 5/10: Passed Senate 36-0, in Assembly.

SB 1216 (Cabaldon) Allows cities that build lots of housing to opt out of some state housing laws. Eligibility is based on being in the top 10% of permits per 1000 people in jurisdictions with lower housing costs, top 5% in higher costs areas, and top 2.5% in the highest cost areas. Status 5/10: Did not make it out of committee.

New Zoning Restrictions

AB 1674 (Ahrens) Requires replacement of grocery stores if no others nearby. Status 5/10: Did not make it out of committee.

AB 2415 (Hoover) Allows smaller cities with multiple SB79 zones near transit to reduce zoning in some already designated historic districts. Status 5/10: Assembly Floor

AB 2576 (Harabedian) Allows historic sites designated on the State Historic Resources Inventory before 1/1/2025 to be exempted from SB79. Currently only local register sites exempt. Status 5/10: Passed Assembly 75–0, in Assembly.

SB 979 (Strickland) Subjects RHNA housing goals to judicial review. Status 5/10: Did not make it out of committee.

SB 1229 (Allen) Limits coastal permit exemption for rebuilding after a disaster to pre-disaster owners. Status 5/10: Senate Floor.

SB 1326 (Wahab) Expanded protection for tribal cultural resources during CEQA. Status 5/10: Senate Appropriations.

SB 1383 (Arreguin) Bans use of density bonus incentives and concessions to remove labor standards. Status 5/10: Senate Floor.
