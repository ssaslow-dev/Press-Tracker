JUDGE DECLARES MISTRIAL IN LINDSAY CLANCY MURDER CASE ⚖️

“At this point I’m going to declare that the jury is deadlocked, and i’m going to declare mistrial”.
Those were the words Judge William F. Sullivan declared today in court, as the Lindsay Clancy murder case has concluded with a mistrial, after jurors — after seven days of deliberations, could not come to a verdict, and remained deadlocked.
This case slowly took the nation by storm, leaked over into culture , politics, and even into average family households. For weeks people have been glued to their favorite court case stream or news source, awaiting the results of this case. Today, the nation got its answer. However the case will not end here, as a mistrial does not mean Lindsay Clancy is ‘not guilty’ or ‘guilty’, and the case will be retried. Lindsay Clancy will remain in custody, and the next hearing has been set for September 29th.
Unfortunately, if you have grown tired of this case, and although it has come to a close, it doesn’t mean it’s over, and another one is set to begin from square one. JERUSALEM POST ✡️: JOIN THE U.S. MILITARY, FIGHT AGAINST IRAN, AND GET YOUR DEBT FORGIVEN?

An opinion column published Aug. 30, 2026, in The Jerusalem Post by A.J. Jaff, a retired Canadian Armed Forces officer who writes on security and geopolitics, has sparked controversy and argued that months of U.S. airstrikes had not ended Iran’s institutional capacity to fight and that a ground campaign, if pursued, would require far more troops than the active U.S. Army currently fields.

Jaff proposed, among other options, a federally run program that would discharge student-loan, credit-card, and other consumer debts after 24 or 36 months of service and extend GI Bill benefits, saying young Americans carrying high non-mortgage debt were the cohort the Pentagon would need. The piece became controversial because critics said it treated household debt as a recruitment lever for a large-scale war in Iran, framed Americans as an expendable manpower pool, and appeared in an Israeli newspaper amid a U.S.-Israel campaign against Iran. Jaff presented the idea as an incentive rather than a draft and listed it alongside proposals such as recognizing Iranian minority independence and a U.S.-China arrangement over Iranian oil.

The wider conflict that began with joint U.S. and Israeli strikes earlier in 2026 has included periods of intense bombing, a June memorandum of understanding that later lapsed, renewed missile and drone exchanges, disruption around the Strait of Hormuz, and disputed civilian casualties, including a Sept. 1 wedding-site strike in southern Iran that U.S. officials said they were investigating. As of early September 2026, fighting had again eased into intermittent strikes and political statements rather than a declared full ground invasion, with U.S. officials describing major combat as having ended after about six weeks while Iran continued limited retaliation and oil and shipping markets remained volatile.

FEDERAL AGENTS ARREST SOCAL MAN FOR MAKING THREATS AGAINST PRESIDENT TRUMP’S LIFE 🚨

Benjamin Azariah Southworth, a 40-year-old Los Angeles podcaster and former host of the Christian youth program “The Remix” and the podcast “Yass, Jesus!,” was arrested Thursday, Sept. 3, 2026, at his home in the city’s Westlake neighborhood after a federal grand jury indicted him on charges of threatening the president, cyberstalking and making harassing interstate telephone calls.

Prosecutors allege that between January and May 2026 he posted videos on Instagram and other platforms calling for President Donald Trump’s assassination, including a March 31 clip in which he said “Kill Donald Trump,” and later depicted a knife attack in the Oval Office; they also accuse him of publishing the home address and phone numbers of Trump’s personal attorney and contacting the attorney’s family. First Assistant U.S. Attorney Bill Essayli said his office “will not tolerate threats of political violence in the United States” and would prosecute anyone who crosses that line “to the fullest extent of the law,” while Attorney General Todd Blanche said the Justice Department would respond with the full force of the law as threats against the president escalate. Southworth pleaded not guilty at his initial appearance the same day. Trump has faced multiple documented attempts and plots in recent years, including the July 13, 2024, rally shooting in Butler, Pennsylvania, in which Thomas Matthew Crooks wounded Trump in the ear and killed a spectator; the September 2024 incident at Trump International Golf Club in West Palm Beach, Florida, in which Ryan Wesley Routh was later convicted and sentenced to life; and an April 25, 2026, armed breach at the White House Correspondents’ Dinner in which a gunman fired at officers before being subdued.

Additional reported incidents include a February 2026 armed intrusion at Mar-a-Lago while Trump was in Washington and intelligence warnings of foreign plots, which together have led to heightened Secret Service operations, criminal convictions, and ongoing federal investigations rather than a change in the president’s public schedule.

CALIFORNIA TAXPAYERS MAY SOON FOOT THE BILL FOR GOVERNOR/TOP DEMS SECURITY DETAILS 🚔

California lawmakers on Sept. 1, 2026, the last day of the legislative session, passed Assembly Bill 113, a budget trailer measure authored by Assembly Budget Committee Chair Jesse Gabriel (D-Encino), that adds $20 million from the Motor Vehicle Account—funded by vehicle registration and driver’s license fees—to the California Highway Patrol for security services for current and former state constitutional officers, legislators, judges and their immediate families. A companion measure, Senate Bill 194, would expand existing CHP authority, which already allows protection of current or former constitutional officers when a threat assessment finds it necessary, to cover immediate family members and to keep an assigned level of protection in place for at least six months before reassessment.

Gov. Gavin Newsom’s term ends in January 2027; has not said whether he would request the detail, and security would still depend on a CHP threat analysis rather than being automatic. Existing state law already permits the CHP commissioner to provide physical security for former governors and other constitutional officers, and former Vice President Kamala Harris, a former California attorney general, has used CHP protection after federal coverage ended; the new package is therefore an expansion of funding and family coverage rather than an entirely new category of protection. Republican legislators argued the Motor Vehicle Account earmark was an inappropriate use of driver fees and that campaign accounts could cover personal security, while supporters said rising threats against public officials justified the increase.

Both bills await the governor’s signature, and is most likely to be passed into law.

USS ABRAHAM HITS THAILAND IN FIRST PORT CALL AFTER 280 DAYS AT SEA IN IRAN CONFLICT 🇹🇭

The Nimitz-class aircraft carrier USS Abraham Lincoln (CVN-72) left its home port of San Diego on Nov. 21, 2025, for what was expected to be a roughly six-month Indo-Pacific cruise and was later redirected to the Middle East for combat operations and a maritime blockade tied to the U.S. campaign against Iran. By early September 2026 the ship had been away from home for about 286 days, including a modern-record stretch of more than 260 consecutive days at sea after only brief earlier stops in Guam and for supplies in Oman.

The deployment drew national attention after families, lawmakers and military news outlets reported supply shortages, plumbing and hygiene problems, and mental-health strain, including at least one sailor recovered after going overboard; Pentagon and White House officials said some accounts were overstated while Democratic senators sought more information. USS George Washington relieved the Lincoln in the Middle East in mid-August, and the Lincoln strike group then sailed east. On Sept. 2, 2026, the carrier made its first full liberty port call at Laem Chabang, Thailand, near Pattaya, for several days of rest and replenishment with no joint operations planned.

It’s being reported that the ship is then proceeding home to San Diego, a Pacific transit that typically takes about a month.
