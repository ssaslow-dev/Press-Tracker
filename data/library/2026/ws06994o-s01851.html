Censoring Public Education: Ethnic Studies & AB 715

On the Ethnic Studies front, Senator Wiener has actively worked to undermine teaching about Palestine and Israel and Arab American history, and has repeatedly conflated the political ideology of Zionism with the religion of Judaism.

Sharing the actual history of Palestinian murder and displacement, and the experience of people impacted by Israel’s apartheid government and military occupation of Palestinian territories, has been mischaracterized as promoting anti-Jewish bias and antisemitism in schools.

The result has been an inordinate amount of pressure gutting the Ethnic Studies curriculum which has had a real impact across the state, including the Pajaro Valley Unified School District, and the San Francisco Unified School District which faced attacks in both 2025 and 2026 in the form of organized efforts to get rid of the ethnic studies requirement altogether, targeting its leadership, and creating a chilling environment for all teachers who are attempting to address one of the most pressing issues of our time — Israeli’s genocide in Gaza — in the classroom.

Although Senator Wiener has refrained from publicly commenting on the most recent Ethnic Studies fights in San Francisco, he has made his feelings and preferences clear to district officials, including to the office of the Superintendent and elected school board members. Those preferences included pausing the curriculum altogether, removing the mandate for it, and reducing the year-long requirement to a semester during the summer 2025 fight. His pressure on the Ethnic Studies fight locally was only put on pause once he started to actively campaign for congress and doing so became a liability for him.

The reality of the political situation is that California’s Israel Lobby has shifted to attacking the Ethnic Studies program at the local level, school district by school district because they have failed at derailing the program at the state level.

The most recent attempt to undermine the Ethnic Studies program via state legislation, AB 1468, failed in May 2025. Senator Wiener was a co-author on the bill.

AB 1468 was drafted by the Legislative Jewish Caucus, which Senator Wiener was co-chair of at the time, without input from the legislature’s other diversity caucuses. It amassed significant opposition, including from the California Teachers Association, and it failed to garner support of the diversity caucuses — namely the Black, Latino, and AAPI caucuses which represent the four foundational groups in the core Ethnic Studies curriculum.

The failure of AB 1468 led to the introduction of AB 715, the pro-Israel public education censorship bill marketed by the Legislative Jewish Caucus as a bill to address antisemitism in K-12 public education. Senator Wiener was a principal co-author of AB 715 and actively engineered its passage through the legislature.

A diverse statewide coalition of more than 120 organizations representing labor, civil rights organizations, and a diverse array of identity groups — including California ACLU Action, the California Teachers Association, and the California Federation of Teachers — formed to oppose AB 715 over concerns that the bill attempted censor education about Israel’s genocide in Palestine and Palestinian representation and narratives — effectively weaponizing accusations of antisemitism to chill speech within the classroom.

Despite overwhelming grassroots opposition, the legislature passed a watered down version of AB 715 (a previous version of the bill prohibited calling Israel a “settler-colonial state” among other extreme Zionist amendments), it was signed into law by Governor Gavin Newsom, and went into effect on January 1, 2026.

The law is the first of its kind in the nation and sets a dangerous precedent designed to be replicated state-by-state to censor criticism of Israel and education about Palestine.
