Election 2020: Incumbent Gabriel running unopposed for Assembly District 45 seat

Staff report

Of the 24 state Assembly districts within and overlapping Los Angeles County, only two districts had a Republican sitting in a legislative seat when voting started this year. Here’s a look at early results for the California state Assembly races that touch LA County as voters headed to the polls on Tuesday, March 3:

District 45: Incumbent Jesse Gabriel, a Democrat attorney who has filed lawsuits against President Donald Trump’s administration in connection to the Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals, is running unopposed.

Gabriel was elected in 2018 to represent the district, which includes much of the west San Fernando Valley.
