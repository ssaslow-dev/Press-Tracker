Meet Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel

Spectrum News Staff

California Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel (D-45) describes his district as "beautiful, vibrant, and fun."

In his Know Your Electeds interview, Gabriel talks about his work with the DACA program and how it inspired him to run for office. He also gives a shoutout to some groups doing important work with people experiencing homelessness. 

Gabriel has represented California's 45th Assembly District since 2018. If he wasn't in public service, he says he'd be a professional soccer player, or just watching a lot of soccer on TV.
