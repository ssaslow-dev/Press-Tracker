Assembly District 45: Gabriel Wins Reelection

Incumbent Jesse Gabriel beat challenger Jeffi Girgenti 67-33 percent, the AP has reported.

Michael Wittner, Patch Staff

Posted Wed, Nov 4, 2020 at 9:49 am PT

NORTHRIDGE, CA — Jesse Gabriel has won reelection 67-33 percent to represent Assembly District 45, which runs from Encino to Calabasas and encompasses most of the West Valley. AP called the race for Gabriel early Wednesday morning.

Neither campaign has released a statement.

Gabriel, a 39-year-old Encino resident, is a constitutional and general litigation attorney elected to the State Assembly in June 2018. He currently serves as Assistant Majority Whip, and is also Chair of the Select Committee on Jobs and Innovation in the San Fernando Valley, and a member of the Select Committee on Women's Reproductive Health.

Gabriel has championed efforts to address California's housing and homelessness crisis, strengthen public education, fight climate change, pass gun safety measures, and protect vulnerable communities. He has also fought to increase COVID testing, provide PPE to healthcare workers, and provide emergency financial relief to small businesses.

Girgenti is a small business owner who won in a write-in contest against Gabriel. She lists repealing AB5, increasing penalties for the mentally ill, protecting Proposition 13, and supporting charter schools as her top priorities.
