AB5 kills jobs in California, making products and services harder to find and more expensive Guest Voice . Guest Voice.

by David Skale What is AB-5?

AB-5 is a new California law that requires most independent contractors to become employees by those who hire them. Why is AB-5 bad?

Many businesses cannot afford the added costs of up to 40% required when hiring an employee.

Thus, AB-5 is bad for business.

Many independent workers lose their freedom when they are forced to become employees even though they don’t want to. Thus, AB-5 is bad for workers.

And many businesses and services that YOU enjoy are fighting to survive RIGHT NOW because of AB-5. They will either have to pass their added costs on to you or they will have to shut down.

Thus, AB-5 is bad for YOU.
