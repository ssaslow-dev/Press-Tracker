Agoura Hills mayor and mayor pro tem announce reelection bid

on June 03, 2022


By John Loesing
newstip@theacorn.com

Deborah Klein Lopez

Deborah Klein Lopez, Agoura Hills City Council member and current mayor, announced she will seek reelection in the Nov. 8 general election.

Lopez was elected to the five-member council in 2018. She is seeking her second term in office.

“The past four years have not been easy ones,” said the former Las Virgenes Unified School District board member and current field representative for 45th District Assembly member Jesse Gabriel (D-Woodland Hills).

Chris Anstead

“Two days after I was elected, the Woolsey fire tore through our region, followed by floods, and then a global pandemic,” she said. “I’m proud to have been a leader and dedicated public servant during these trying times.”

Lopez and fellow incumbent Chris Anstead are up for reelection on the November ballot. The seat of Denis Weber, the city’s longest-serving council member, is also in line for reelection, but Weber told The Acorn he is undecided about his plans for the fall.

Anstead announced he is running.

“I have enjoyed serving the residents of Agoura Hills and look forward to continuing to serve them,” said the City Council member and mayor-to-be who first came into office in 2018.

Anstead is a government accounts executive and a West Point graduate.

Jeremy Wolf, district director for state Sen. Henry Stern and chair of the Agoura Hills Planning Commission, threw his hat into the ring at the end of May and is the only challenger in the race so far.

Lopez laid out her platform for the fall campaign.

“(I am) proud to have maintained focus on the issues that I first campaigned upon: environmental sustainability, responsible development, public health and safety, communication, open space connectivity and more,” she said.

Lopez said highlights from her first term include: Leading Agoura Hills to 100% renewable energy procurement; increasing the city’s presence on social media platforms; producing monthly newsletters for the community; helping to create and subsequently serving on the Agoura Village Specific Plan Citizen’s Advisory Group; meeting with businesses to help with COVID-19 guidelines; and maintaining four years of balanced budgets, even during the pandemic.

City Council members serve four-year terms. The position of mayor rotates among the officeholders.
