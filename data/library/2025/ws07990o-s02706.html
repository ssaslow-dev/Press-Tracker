Newsom expands special session intended to 'Trump proof' California to propose billions in aid for LA

The funding would help with reopening schools impacted by the fires and additional wildfire preparedness.

By Staff (ABC10)
Published 4:13 PM PST January 13, 2025

LOS ANGELES — A session initially focused on efforts to protect California values amid a second administration under president-elect Donald Trump was expanded to address the Los Angeles wildfires.

The call from Gov. Gavin Newsom comes amid the devastation wrought by the wildfires in Los Angeles County, specifically the Eaton and Palisades fires, which are among the most destructive wildfires in state history. More than 12,000 structures have been destroyed.

Newsom is proposing at least $2.5 billion in additional funding for emergency response and recovery efforts for the area, such as reopening schools impacted by the fires and additional wildfire preparedness.

“California is organizing a Marshall Plan to help Los Angeles rebuild faster and stronger – including billions in new and accelerated state funding so we can move faster to deliver for the thousands who’ve lost their homes and livelihoods in these firestorms. To the people of Los Angeles: We have your back,” Newsom said in a news release.

The fire has already seen a historic response from first responders, with more than 15,000 personnel battling the fires. Additional pre-positioning of resources is also underway due to another round of fire weather in the forecast.

“We appreciate the Governor's commitment to providing funding, resources, and support to help rebuild our Southern California communities. This is deeply personal for our LA members and the communities we represent," Asm. Jesse Gabriel (D-Encino). "The Assembly looks forward to working closely with our state, federal, and local partners to ensure that we are supporting impacted communities every step of the way. As we navigate extremely dangerous conditions this week, we remain profoundly grateful to the firefighters and first responders who are protecting our homes and families.”

Not everyone is on board with including Los Angeles relief in a special session focused on the incoming Trump administration, though. Republican lawmakers asked Newsom to convene a separate special session for the wildfires. The following is a statement from Senate Minority Leader Brian Jones (R-San Diego):

“I’m encouraged that Governor Newsom heard our calls to take action on the LA wildfires in a special session.

 However, lumping this emergency response together with a politically motivated special session to sue Trump is unnecessary and, frankly, insulting to those struggling through this disaster. Fire victims deserve a response that is both respectful and non-partisan, but instead, Newsom is politicizing a tragic crisis that should focus solely on aiding those in need. Sadly, these special session bills will likely include inappropriate provisions targeting the incoming federal administration alongside vital disaster relief for Californians.

A better and more thoughtful approach would have been to convene a new special session exclusively dedicated to investigating the LA fires, supporting communities in recovery, and ensuring we are better prepared for future disasters.

Nevertheless, Republicans remain committed to working with our Democrat colleagues to secure wildfire relief and deliver real solutions for all Californians.”
