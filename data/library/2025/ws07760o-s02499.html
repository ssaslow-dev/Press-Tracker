Assemblymembers Davies and Wallis Join Armenian Legislative Caucus

by Asbarez Staff June 19, 2025

in Featured Story, Latest, News, Top Stories

0

Assemblymembers Laurie Davies and Greg Wallis

SACRAMENTO—The California Armenian Legislative Caucus Foundation announced that Assemblymembers Laurie Davies (R-Laguna Niguel) and Greg Wallis (R-Bermuda Dunes) have recently joined the group.

“I am honored to join the California Armenian Legislative Caucus Foundation. California is home to the largest population of Armenian Americans. Each year we celebrate April 24th, the anniversary of the Armenian Genocide, when the Ottoman Empire commenced the systematic murder of more than 1.5 million Armenians,” Davies said.

“I stand with the other legislative members in the California Armenian Legislative Caucus Foundation to recognize that Armenians continue to face an existential threat at the hands of Turkey and Azerbaijan. We know that more still needs to be done to protect Armenia’s sovereignty and the freedom of its people,” she added.

“I am proud to join the California Armenian Legislative Caucus Foundation. I have been a longtime supporter of Armenian American issues in my district, and I look forward to amplifying Armenian American voices at the statewide level.” Wallis noted.

In addition to Wallis and Davis, the Legislative Caucus includes: Chair, Assemblymember John Harabedian; Senate President pro Tempore Senator Mike McGuire; senators Bob Archuleta, Megan Dahle, María Elena Durazo, Caroline Menjivar, Sasha Renée Pérez, Eloise Gómez Reyes, Laura Richardson, Susan Rubio and Suzette Martinez Valladares; Speaker Robert Rivas; and Assemblymembers Dawn Addis, Cecilia Aguiar-Curry, Patrick Ahrens, Juan Alanis, Rebecca Bauer-Kahan, Marc Berman, Tasha Boerner, Lisa Calderon, Jessica Caloza, Stan Ellis, Mike Fong, Jesse Gabriel, Robert Garcia, Mike Gipson, Jeff Gonzalez, Mark Gonzalez, Heather Hadwick, Matt Haney, Maggy Krell, Tom Lackey, Alex Lee, Alexandra Macedo, Stephanie Nguyen, Liz Ortega, Blanca Pacheco, Diane Papan, Darshana Patel, Gail Pellerin, Cottie Petrie-Norris, Celeste Rodriguez, Michelle Rodriguez, Chris Rogers, Blanca Rubio, Pilar Schiavo, Nick Schultz, José Luis Solache, Esmeralda Soria, Catherine Stefani, Tri Ta, David Tangipa, Avelino Valencia, and Rick Chavez Zbur.

The California Armenian Legislative Caucus Foundation serves as a forum for members from the California Senate and Assembly to identify key issues affecting Armenian Americans and develop and empower the Armenian American community throughout California. The Foundation encourages advocacy and participation in cultural, educational, and community efforts in California. Through advocacy, the Foundation strives to ensure that California Armenian American’s voices are heard and given a platform.
