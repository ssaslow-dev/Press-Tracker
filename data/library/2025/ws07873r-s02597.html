Bill aims to phase out "particularly harmful" ultra-processed foods from California public schools

This week, as evidence mounts of the health impacts of ultra-processed foods, California Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel (D-Encino) introduced legislation that would phase out certain foods from being served in the state's public schools. Gabriel joined Courtney Friel to discuss the proposal, the road ahead, and the growing bipartisan push to make children, and others, healthier. March 20, 2025.
