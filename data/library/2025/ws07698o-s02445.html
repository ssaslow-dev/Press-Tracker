Whitewashing Genocide: The California Legislative Jewish Caucus

Nadia Rahman

We are at the point in the Gaza genocide that we need to memorialize everyone - leaders, politicians, institutions - who cheered on war and genocide in Gaza over the last two years even as they change their tune as polling showing support for Israel among Democrats is at all-time lows.

A Gallup poll from just last month reported that only 8 percent of Democrats in the United States indicated support of Israel’s military action in Gaza (aka Israel’s genocide in Gaza).

It’s in this context, that the California Legislative Jewish Caucus shared an exclusive in Politico’s California Playbook yesterday that it made a $25,000 donation to “groups that provide humanitarian aid to Palestinians in Gaza.”

However, this donation is the California Legislative Jewish Caucus playing in the face of the Palestine solidarity activists and organizers who it vehemently demonized and feverishly worked against for the last two years since Israel’s genocide in Gaza began in October 2023.

The California Legislative Jewish Caucus has a long list of sins related to its active enablement of and complicity in the Gaza genocide. It is chaired by two die hard Zionist legislators - Senator Scott Wiener and Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel - and has spent two solid years:

  • Demonizing Palestine advocates and demonstrations, inclusive of anti-Zionist Jews who are excluded from being represented by the Caucus.

  • Working aggressively against local ceasefire resolutions.

  • Demonizing Palestine solidarity on campuses and the encampment movement.

  • Has gotten at least one university administrator, Mike Lee of Sonoma State, fired over agreeing to divestment from Israel and by doing so, sent a clear message to other administrators that divestment will cost them their jobs.

  • And has championed bills attacking public education over Israel in California (SB 1287, AB 1468, AB 715)

SB 1287 - an attempt to criminalize pro-Palestine campus protests and ended up being a campus censorship bill chilling free speech.

AB 1468 - an attack to undermine the statewide Ethnic Studies curriculum. 

AB 715 - a pro-Israel public education censorship bill which is the California Legislative Jewish Caucus’s only legislative priority for the year. Specific members of the Caucus (Zbur, Wiener and Gabriel) are all named in Lily Greenberg Call’s op-ed in The New York Times (titled: “California Democrats Are Fighting Trump’s Battle for Him”).

The California Legislative Jewish Caucus is fully aligned with California’s pro-Israel Lobby which includes the Jewish Public Affairs Committee of California (JPAC), the Jewish Community Relations Council (JCRC), and the Anti-Defamation League (ADL).

We need to call out this whitewashing as it arises and everyone begins to pretend they were always against the genocide in Gaza, especially those who need to be held accountable for cheering it on — including the California Legislative Jewish Caucus.

They want us to forget but we cannot; there is no justice without accountability and they must be held accountable for their roles in:

  • Helping shield Israel from criticism as it committed genocide in Gaza.

  • Being pro-war and justifying the bombings of civilians. 

  • Laying the foundation for Trump to weaponize antisemitism on college campuses in the battles we are now seeing play out at Harvard and UCLA. 

And no, the California Legislative Jewish Caucus has not yet admitted that Israel’s actions in Gaza are genocide despite widespread consensus that it indeed is.

This is a message for the Caucus: Y’all are not going to be able to sidestep accountability and act like you did not say and do damaging things, pass problematic legislation, and cheer on a genocide that you insist on calling a war.
