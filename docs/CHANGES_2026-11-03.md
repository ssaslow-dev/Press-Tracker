  - **4 new searches every run** (a priority group can now carry its own `query` template):
    - World Journal and Sing Tao;

  - **Not added, with reasons:**
    - Direct searches of Spanish outlets: 68 hits, 67 already tracked.
    - TinyFish `language=` filters: they don't filter; native words in the query do.
    - Persian outlet searches: noise.
  - Default plan: priority searches go from 6 to 10, about 741 calls in all.
- **DATEFILTER-v1.** TinyFish's `after_date` drops results it has no date for.
  - The podcast-page OR-of-sites query returned 0 with any date and 10 without. That group is now marked `nodate`, and old results are dropped afterwards by the since date.
  - Every other search shape was tested and keeps its date; the date helps them.
  - Search-input building moved into a new `tfInput()` in `41_update_search.js`.
  - Separately, the spellings test found that `after_date`/`before_date` with `include_domains` in web mode often returns 0, while `domain_type=news` honors dates.
- **URL dedupe (extends YTLINK-v1).** `norm_url` now keeps the query string for iranshahrnewsagency.com (`?ItemId=`) and chosundaily.com (`?wr_id=`). It is changed in `sheet_task.py`, `build_data.py` and `40_update_outlets.js` (`covNormU`). Without it, two Iranshahr items deduped as one.
- **Outlets.** 8 new records in `outlets.json` and `outlet_rules.json`: World Journal, Sun Media, Iranshahr News Agency (location unknown, so Reach is blank), Shafaqna, News.am, Massis Post, Chosun Daily (US) and KBS News.
- **`release_related.json`.** The save run's 8 new links were merged in by union.
- **Test fix.** `test_since_js` now exempts the undated podcast-page group, matched by `site:open.spotify.com/episode`, from the "every search carries after_date" check.
