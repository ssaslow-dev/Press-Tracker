# Bills (bill_timeline)
- Shows: a picker (every bill with coverage, most covered first; default = most-covered authored bill, AB 418) and a chart of stories per week (Monday weeks, Originals in ctx.D) with leginfo milestones. Introduced, floor and concurrence votes and the final action (signed, vetoed, chaptered without a signature, died) are dashed rules. Committee votes are dots. Labels are drawn for Introduced, the final floor vote and the final action; every milestone has a tooltip and is listed under "Show the milestones". Toggle: All dates / Session only. Click a week to list its stories (opens the story panel). Footer links: See all in Clips ({bills}), leginfo history. Below that, a table "Bills at a glance" (bill + session, title, final status, stories, peak week). It shows the top 12 rows, with "Show all" for the rest; clicking a row charts that bill. Params: `bill`, `s` (session, only when a number repeats), `range=session`.
- Data: ctx.D, ctx.bills(), and block `ins-bill_timeline` from bill_timeline_data.py. Sources: a "Bills" sheet tab if one exists (columns Bill, Session, Date, Milestone, Detail, optional Final status), otherwise L/work/out/bill_milestones.json (agent-billstatus). The block is 124 KB, covers 273 bills and is deterministic. With no source the build returns None, and the view says "Milestones not loaded yet". In the pipeline the JSON path is outside `ctx`, so a refresh drops the block unless the Bills tab exists. The durable home is therefore a Bills tab with the columns above (coordinator's sheet write).
- Session rule (same as agent-billstatus): a story goes to the latest session of that bill number that started (Dec 1 of the prior year) on or before the story date.
- Not plotted (but counted in the table and listed on click): stories dated only to month or year, and stories dated before the bill's session.
- Story counts are Originals in the tracker. AB 418 = 422. agent-billstatus's tracker_stories (429) counts Coverage rows, which also include non-tracker rows.
- Final status comes from the block's leginfo final_status, shortened (e.g. "Signed into law", "Died in committee (APPR)", "Pending"). Without it, the billsinfo status is used.
- Block drops 'other' steps from the chart: suspense file, enrolled, passed both houses, and chaptered on the signature day. These steps still appear in the milestone table.
- Test: `SRV=../test_bill_timeline/srv MOD=bill_timeline node harness.js` passes 60/60 at both viewports. The private srv copy is needed because test/srv/index.html embeds a stale fixture `ins-bill_timeline` block, which shadows the injected one. Rebuild the test page to clear it.
