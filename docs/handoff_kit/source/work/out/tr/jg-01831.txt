A Democrat and a Republican defend California's rainy-day fund

Politics and Government · October 13, 2026 · Source: CalMatters

Assemblymembers Jesse Gabriel, a Democrat, and Josh Hoover, a Republican, argue that Proposition 2 is not about partisan politics but about fiscal responsibility. They note that California ranks fifth in the nation in revenue volatility but only 29th in the size of its budget reserves.

Why it matters: The op-ed illustrates a rare bipartisan agreement on how states can prepare for economic downturns, a model for governing amid volatility.
