HIGHLIGHT | COMIDA DO AMANHÃ INSTITUTE

This week's highlight from the Comida do Amanhã Institute is the third official launch of the Agroecological PNAE program, held March 26–27 in the municipalities taking part in the initiative in the state of Pará: Benevides, Barcarena, Abaetetuba and Marituba.

On Thursday the 26th, the event took place at the Benevides City Council, bringing together public officials, farmers, technical staff and representatives of partner organizations. On Friday the 27th, the program moved on to Barcarena with another formal presentation of the program, plus two technical visits: to the kitchen of the host school and to a community, to see first-hand the experience of a producer who benefits from the project.

One of the Agroecological PNAE's goals is to strengthen family farming and improve school meals through the National School Feeding Program (PNAE). It is run nationally by the Comida do Amanhã Institute in partnership with the Fome Zero Institute (IFZ), the World Food Programme's (WFP) Centre of Excellence against Hunger in Brazil, and the Rockefeller Foundation. In Pará, the initiative is supported by the Regenera Institute, Terra Preta and the municipal governments of Benevides, Barcarena, Abaetetuba and Marituba.

HIGHLIGHT | FOME ZERO INSTITUTE

Today's IFZ highlight is an article by Jorge Meza, FAO Representative in Brazil, published on our blog.

Each year the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) produces the global report on food security and nutrition, in which it publishes the prevalence of undernourishment. This figure indicates levels of hunger in the world, in countries and in regions. The 2025 report finds that 8.5% of the world's population is undernourished, that is, hungry. And there are important data linking hunger to income levels. FAO is present in more than 190 countries, and within the cooperation it offers, the question is: how to advance the organization's mandate in light of countries' practical experience? How to eradicate hunger?
