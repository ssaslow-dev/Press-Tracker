## Identity
- Jesse Samuel Gabriel, Democrat, born 1981-09-25 in Berkeley. Married to Rachel Rosner, with three sons. Source: Wikipedia.
- **AD 45:** won the 2018-06-05 special election with 65.7%; served until 2022. **AD 46:** since 2022-12-05; won the 2024 race with 62.9%; on the 2026-11-03 ballot. Sources: Ballotpedia, Wikipedia.
- **Education:** Oak Park HS 2000; UC Berkeley BA 2004 (ASUC president 2002–03); Harvard JD 2008. Sources: Wikipedia; UC Berkeley NewsCenter 2004-05-10.
- **Before office:** counsel to Sen. Evan Bayh (2008–10); Gibson Dunn, including the DACA litigation; Daily Journal "California Lawyer Attorney of the Year" 2018. Sources: Wikipedia; Spectrum "Know Your Electeds" 2020-07-21.
