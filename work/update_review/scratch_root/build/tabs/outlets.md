# Outlets tab (agent-tab-outlets, TABS-v1, 2026-10-27)
**Shows:** one row per canonical outlet (ctx.outlet; spelling variants merged, listed as "Also listed as"). Units = what an outlet ran: each tracker story in its own outlet (original) + once per other outlet its reprints (r.reps; copies in the story's own outlet and repeat copies in one reprint outlet are not counted again; a reprint is dated by its earliest copy, else the story's date). Story filters cut units before grouping, so counts / tone / last story follow them.
**Row:** outlet (+ city or where · reach · format · non-English language), publisher, stories, originals, reprints, last story, top 3 issues, tone avg (ctx.tone over rated stories, originals + reprints), negative (Negative + Very negative), top 3 reporters. CSV adds where, county, state, city, reach, format, language, rated n, first story, spellings, website.
**Filters:** date range (unit), issue, publisher at the time of the story, language, ran as (original/reprint), story tone (`tmix`), county or state (`geo`), reach (`rch`), story count (`n`), tone average (`tavg`), active within (`active`). No default date filter (all time).
**Publisher:** sheet Publisher spans (ctx.publishers); a multi-owner outlet shows "Tegna 2019–2026; Nexstar Media Group 2026". A story's publisher = the span covering its date, else the last owner before it (KTLA 2019-08 -> Tribune Media; ctx.publisherOf would pick the biggest span). Santa Maria Times / Lompoc Record / Santa Ynez Valley News: every row in the sheet is dated after 2020-03-13 and reads Santa Maria News Media (no Lee rows left); tested against the workbook.
**Outlet page** #outlets/<slug>: facts = publisher, stories, originals, reprints, tone average, negative, last/first story (open the story panel), top issues, reporters (links to #reporters/<ctx.slug(name)>, top 10 + "and N others"), where, reach, format, language, website (most common publisher-link host, with copy icon). Chart and Clips-style list from the framework over the outlet's distinct stories. Note: list rows show the story's ORIGINAL outlet/date (framework row), so reprints on e.g. the LA Daily News page read "Pasadena Star-News"; the chart counts stories by original date.
**Reporters:** people who wrote the outlet's ORIGINAL stories (same rules as Reporters/Pitch: people only, author titles and Jesse Gabriel out).
**Data:** ctx.D, tab-common, and `tab_outlets` (outlets_data.py, 56 KB): the sheet's most common Reach / Medium / Language / county / state / city per canonical outlet (reprint rows in data.json have no reach/format/language). Fallback: the tracker's own story fields.
**Slugs:** ctx.slug(name); 2 collisions ("India West"/"India-West", "Food and/& Environment Reporting Network") get "-2" (framework name cleaning does not merge punctuation variants).
**Test:** `MOD=outlets node harness.js` 132/132 (66 at 1400, 66 at 390). Screenshots: work/tmp_agent-tab-outlets/shots/outlets_{list,list_dark,page,page_dark,page_full}_{1400,390}.png, outlets_filters_390.png.
**Bill links (addendum 2026-10-27 23:50):** the Outlets module renders no bill numbers itself (rows and page facts show issues, not bills), so nothing to route through ctx.billLink. The only bill numbers on outlet pages are the bill chips in the framework story list (`.bchip[data-bill]`), which the integrator upgrades with ctx.billLink.
