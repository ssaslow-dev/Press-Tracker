# Story travel (agent-ins-story_travel)
Shows: (1) "How far a story traveled": pick a story (Source + Story selects; defaults to the most reprinted, or `params.id` from "Trace this story"). The original (ringed, at day 0) and every dated reprint are dots on a days-after-the-original axis, stacked per day and coloured by the reprinting outlet's primary Reach. Undated reprints and reprints dated before the original get a note. "Show the numbers" lists all of them. (2) "Where it ran": `ctx.drawCountyMap` for California home counties (hidden when no CA county), plus states/countries outside CA. (3) "Top sources": originals' outlets ranked by reprints, distinct reprinting outlets, stories reprinted; "By newsroom group" merges SCNG and Bay Area News Group papers. Clicking a source narrows the story picker.
Data: ctx.D (reps: outlet/date/county/state) + block `ins-story_travel` (24.9 KB) from story_travel_data.py, which gives each reprint a Reach: the row's own sheet Reach (10 overrides), else same-outlet consensus, else same-publisher consensus, else inferred from the state (non-CA US state = Out-of-state local, foreign = International; 213 outlets, drawn hollow and labelled "inferred"), else "Not classified" (about 330 of 2,819 reprints). Without the block, the view falls back to outlet consensus from originals.
Assumptions/flags for Sammy: sources merge case variants (CALmatters/CalMatters) and AP wire names into "Associated Press". The SCNG/BANG membership lists are hand-written in the module. The state-based Reach inference is a guess, which is why those dots are drawn hollow. 61 reprints are dated before their original (print edition vs web), and they are shown left of day 0.
Test: `MOD=story_travel node harness.js` 46/46 PASS at 1400 and 375. Render about 10 ms after the memo is built. Screenshots: test_story_travel/shot_*.png.
