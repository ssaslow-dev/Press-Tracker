Assemblymember Gabriel's press coverage, in tables

Everything the office's press tracker holds, for readers who will never open it  ·  Data as of Nov 5, 2026

What the tracker is

The tracker is the office's database of every news story that names Assemblymember Gabriel: print, online, TV, radio, podcasts and non-English outlets. Stories are found by web searches run with Claude (an AI assistant), monthly downloads from the NewsBank and ProQuest newspaper archives, and TV transcripts. Each story is checked to confirm it is about him. It is then tagged with its outlet, date, topic, bills, tone and how prominently he appears.

A story is the first appearance of a piece of reporting. When other outlets run the same text, those copies are counted as reprints of that story, not as new stories. When a site posts one of his press releases word for word, that is a release copy and is not counted as coverage.
