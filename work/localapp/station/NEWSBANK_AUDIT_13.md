# Audit 13: the Update station on Sammy's real batch multi4.pdf (spot check, 2026-11-04)

Independent, read-only judgment review. Scope was cut by Sammy to a spot check: a handful of text stories and the full pages, only what the station gets wrong or has never seen. Method: the PDF read page by page (`pdftotext` plus the page pictures inside it), the for-Update file `for_update_0bccc1fc.json`, the station code in the working tree, and a run of the built page (`dist/index.html`, same sha as a fresh build) with Playwright and a fake runtime: Claude's answers were canned from the TRUE content of the pages, so the station's logic is what was tested, not Claude. No network. Evidence in the scratchpad folder `audit13/` (items, rows, pages docs, the update zip); licensed text stays there. Failing tests for findings 1-3: `tests/review13/nb_real4_test.js` (needs `AJG_REAL_NB4`, skips without it).

## What the batch is

85 pages, 27 NewsBank records: 13 text stories and 14 "Full Page" print pages (not 9). The station reads all 27 correctly: page spans, headlines, dates, sources, sections, word counts, authors and ids all match the PDF, no warnings, no bands. Two text stories are rightly "already in the tracker" (00001 Pasadena Star-News web story s00001; 00003 Daily Breeze print record), eleven are rightly new. Three things in this batch the station had never seen, all handled: a citation link that wraps onto the next page (stories 12, 13, 18: the ids are read right), a Spanish story (18, Excelsior: language Spanish, headline translated), and print-edition text records with `Page:` (10, 13, 17, 25: Edition Print, Date source print edition). Also new and NOT handled: a jump (continuation) page, the same paper under two NewsBank names, and print pages of stories that are in the batch itself or already in the tracker for that very outlet (findings 1-3).

The run proposed 24 rows (15 new stories, 9 reprint rows). The right answer is about 11 new stories, 5 reprint rows and 0 rows from eight of the fourteen pages. Nine of the 24 rows are wrong.

## Findings, ranked

### 1. Print pages of a story the tracker already holds for that outlet become new rows (high)

The six Oct 25 pages (Whittier Daily News, San Gabriel Valley Tribune, Pasadena Star-News, Long Beach Press-Telegram, Daily News, Daily Breeze, all "Page: 14") are one SCNG plate: "Term limits shake up runs for Senate, Assembly" by Linh Tat, which names him under "46th Assembly District". The tracker already has this story as s00001 with a row for every one of the six outlets (00001 Original, 00003-00007 NewsBank print records, p. 14). Nothing new should be proposed: the six page PDFs are the print originals of those rows (`_original_pdf/2026/<aid>_p14.pdf`, PRINTORIG-v1; ORIGCOPIES-v1 "not dropped, saved as an extra original copy of that row").

What happened (run with the headline as printed): a NEW Original story "Whittier Daily News" plus five Reprint rows of it (SGVT, PSN, LBPT, "Daily News of Los Angeles", Daily Breeze), six duplicate rows and a wrong Original; the "known" Daily Breeze text record 00003 sits in the same batch, same outlet, same date, same page 14, and is never connected to its page. With NewsBank's kicker in the headline ("2026 elections - Term limits ..."): six Reprint rows of s00001 "filed as its print edition", one for an outlet that already has that reprint row and one for the Original's own outlet.

Causes. `nbPrintApply` (src/newsbank.js:156-171) matches a reading only by headline (`covHit`, then `dupOf` against `D`, the Originals): the printed headline lacks NewsBank's kicker, so the exact match fails and the 0.8 word overlap needs "same outlet", but `D` holds the Original (Pasadena Star-News), not the reprint rows. When a match is found it always sets `it.confirm` and `nbRun` (line 180) makes a Reprint row with no `ranIn` / `covRepKnown` check (the text-story path at line 67-68 has one). The strong cue the batch gives, a text record with the same outlet, date and `Page:` (00003 p. 14; also finding 2), is not used. Expected: a page whose outlet already has a row for the matched story is "known": extra copy of that row, no row; and the page-to-record link by outlet + date + page.

### 2. The same paper under NewsBank's two names, and pages of stories in the same batch (high)

NewsBank calls the Los Angeles Daily News "Los Angeles Daily News (CA)" on text records and "Daily News of Los Angeles (CA)" on its e-edition pages; the sheet spells it "Los Angeles Daily News" (172 rows). `nbSpell` keys on `covOutlet`, which cannot equate the two, so three rows are filed under a new outlet "Daily News of Los Angeles". Worse, two of those pages are the printed pages of text records in this very batch: Nov 4 p. 9 ("Early mail-in ballots tend to favor incumbents", Teresa Liu) is story 25 (Los Angeles Daily News 2026-11-04, Page: 9), and Oct 27 p. 3 ("Sepulveda Basin gets $22M in funding", Teresa Liu) is story 13 (Los Angeles Daily News 2026-10-27, Page: 3). Each became a SECOND Original row beside the batch's own one: the station matches page readings against the for-Update file only, never against the stories read from the batch, and the step-3 duplicate check (`dupOf` among the new stories) fails because the two outlet strings differ and the headline overlap is 0.78, under 0.9. Expected: one Original each, the page PDF as its print original. (The Sepulveda story is also the print edition of row 10324, Los Angeles Daily News web 2026-10-26, s04914; the station cannot see that and the save's text test decides, as the rules say; noted, not counted.)

### 3. A jump page is thrown to Unlinked print pages with a wrong reason (medium-high)

Oct 27 p. 4 is the continuation of the p. 3 Sepulveda story: slug "BASIN / From Page 3", the paragraph with his quote and a captioned photo of him. Claude's honest reading (headline "BASIN", continued_from "From Page 3", 100+ words) is rejected by `nbpAccept` (src/newsbank_ui.js:91) because the HEADLINE is under 6 characters, and the reason recorded is "too little text to be an article". The page becomes an Unlinked print pages row. Expected: `continued_from` ties it to the p. 3 page of the same outlet and date, kept as `_p3-4` of that story, no Unlinked row. If Claude instead infers the story's headline, the page becomes yet another Original (finding 2).

### 4. The five Non-Ultraprocessed pages: four right, one new story (medium)

East Bay Times Nov 1, Mercury News Nov 3, Oroville Mercury-Register and Enterprise-Record Nov 4 are read and matched to s01903 (the LA Times story): correct Reprint rows, Edition Print, print-page link. Santa Cruz Sentinel Nov 4 carries the same article under the headline "Calif. creates 'Non-Ultraprocessed Certified' food label and standard", same byline "By Karen Garcia, Los Angeles Times": it becomes a NEW Original. Headline-only matching; the byline credit (author + "Los Angeles Times" + date window) is not used. By the rules the save's text test may still fold it in, but a trimmed print version can fail the 90% rule, and the sheet would then hold a new Original for a story it has as s01903 with 8 reprints. Two smaller things on the four right rows: Headline is the LA Times WEB headline ("California just changed the way you shop for food. Here's how", `nbRowFill` line 309 `of_hl`) while the sheet's existing print reprints of s01903 carry the printed headline, and the read byline is dropped.

### 5. File names: page scans are named as article-text copies (medium)

The update folder names every page PDF `library/2026/nb<ID>o|r-s<ID>.pdf` (the names block: rows 3-8, 20-23), the schema for NewsBank TEXT copies. PRINTORIG-v1 names print originals `_original_pdf/<year>/<aid>_p<page>.pdf`, and "View original" and the planned skip-to-his-name bar only open files named that way. NEWSBANK.md 5 says PC patch 09 renames confirmed pages; a new-from-page story (NEWSBANK.md 6: "its saved copy is the page PDF nb<ID>o-s<ID>.pdf") would have no print original at all.
