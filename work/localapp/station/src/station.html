How it works

What this page does

It finds new stories about Jesse Gabriel and packs them into one zip, the update folder. You bring that zip into the PC tracker (Tracker files, Bring in update). The PC tracker shows you everything before it saves. This page never changes your workbook.

Step 1: the “for Update” file

Make it on the PC (Tracker files, Make “for Update” file) and load it here. It lists the stories the tracker already has, so nothing is found twice. It holds no article text and is not stored online.

Make a new one after every save. A file made before a save does not know about the stories that save added, and finds them again.

Step 2: Update

Update searches the web from the day before the newest story in the tracker up to today. There is no date to pick. It takes about 25 minutes; keep this tab open.

Claude reads every result and keeps only real stories that name Assemblymember Gabriel. It leaves out press releases, social posts, YouTube, blogs, listing pages and other people named Gabriel. It fills in each story’s issue, bills, format, reach and tone.

A story the tracker already has is left out, even when the tracker’s copy came from NewsBank and has no web link. A copy of a known story at another outlet is added as a reprint. A row with no date takes the date in its web address when it has one.

Stories not in English get an English headline, and their whole story is translated into English when you download the folder.

NewsBank and ProQuest PDFs

About 1 in 6 stories (print, wires, paywalled papers) can’t be found on the web. About once a month, and soon after big events like the budget vote, download the new NewsBank PDFs as the NewsBank Download Guide shows (State Library login, the saved search, Sort by: Oldest) and drop them in “Add NewsBank PDFs”. ProQuest PDFs work the same way in their own box.

A NewsBank “Full Page” is a picture of a newspaper page. Claude reads it and adds the article about him to the right story, or as a new story. A page it can’t place goes to “Unlinked print pages” in the tracker.

Step 3: Download

Press Download the update folder when you are done. It holds everything found since you loaded the file. If you add more after downloading, download again: the new folder replaces the old one.

The PC tracker makes the final call when it saves: it compares the article texts to decide what is a reprint.

Automatic mode

When it is on, PDFs you drop in are added by themselves. Update and Download are never automatic, and nothing runs on a schedule.

If something is blocked

Claude must be allowed for this page (the Permissions menu), including sending pictures, so it can read print pages. TinyFish must be connected in claude.ai (Settings, Connectors) and allowed for this page.
