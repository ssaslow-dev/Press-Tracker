# Print-page reading (PRINTREAD-v2): will it work on Sammy's next real run?

Independent judgment, 2026-11-05. Read-only: code (`src/newsbank_ui.js`, `src/newsbank.js`, `src/station.js`, the PC's `sheet_task.py` and `reprint_rule.py`), the decisions (PRINTNEW-v1, PRINTREAD-v2 and its tiling note, STFIX-v1, PRINTANY-v1, AUTOSTATION-v1, NORECOVER-v1, REPRINT90-v1), the runtime's `sample` contract (0.2.96), and the evidence in the scratchpad: the real batch `multi4.pdf` (14 full pages, 13 text records), the exact tiles the station sends (`tiles2/`, 2 x 4 per page, 487 x 502-507 px, 15% overlap, about 1 MB a page), the two folders from Sammy's runs, and audit 13 (the true content of each page). Nothing was changed.

## Verdict: likely

The pictures are no longer the problem. I read the tiles myself: body text, bylines, folios and the small lines that name him are legible on every page I checked, including the hard one (the Oct 25 "Term limits" plate, where his name sits in one short paragraph of a two-page candidate list: "46th Assembly District: Democratic Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel, who is seeking a fifth term..."), the East Bay Times line the decision quotes, the jump page's "BASIN / From Page 3" with his quote, and "Democratic Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel, seeking a fifth term, appeared to be ahead" on the Nov 4 page. A tile is 0.24 megapixels, well under the 1.2 megapixels the platform shrinks to, so what is sent is what Claude sees. The first real run failed because the whole page went as one picture (1.6 MP, shrunk) and Claude honestly answered "no article names him" for all 14; that cause is gone.

What still stands between "legible" and "right rows":

1. **A page Claude reads as "no article names him" is thrown to Unlinked print pages even when the batch itself places it.** The fallback that places a page by its own text record (same outlet, date and page number, STFIX 1) runs only for `unclear` / `unavailable` / `error` with nothing seen, not for `none`. Oct 27 p. 3 (Sepulveda Basin) names him on that page only in a photo caption; the body names him on the jump page. If Claude answers "none" (likely under "true only if you can see his name in the text"), the page loses the attachment it had in Sammy's second folder. The same happens to Daily Breeze p. 14 and Nov 4 p. 9 if Claude misses the name in a long page. The batch's text record is stronger evidence than a "no" from a picture.
2. **The 180-second page-side timer.** The runtime contract says not to build one (the platform ends a round at about five minutes; a page timer also counts the time the consent dialog is up). The Term-limits plate is about 1,450 words to transcribe from eight tiles on the default tier (thinks first, then writes 2,000+ tokens): 60-150 s is normal. A timeout aborts a call the viewer has already paid for and turns a readable page into an Unlinked row. In automatic mode the page reading can be the view's first Claude call, so the dialog's time counts too.
3. **The PC's text test is the strict one.** Four of the five "Non-Ultraprocessed" pages become print-edition Reprint rows of s01903 and the Santa Cruz page a new story; on the PC each is decided by the string-of-text rule (25-word runs covering 90% of both texts, or a credited text 90% inside the longer). A Claude transcription of 9-pixel print will have a wrong word every 50-100 words; each breaks a run, and coverage can fall under 90%. Then `check_reprint_rows` turns a right Reprint into a story of its own (`reprint_failed`). The OCR-tolerant verdict (OCRTOL-v1, `reprint_rule.text_verdict_ocr`, edit distance 1) exists but is opt-in and `sheet_task.py` never calls it.
4. **The office-account instructions declare `sample: {}`** (README step 3, MOVE_TO_OFFICE.md). Without `sample: {images: true}` the runtime reports no `images`, every page is "not read" and only a warning line says why. The current private artifact evidently has the declaration (the first run reached Claude with pictures); the copy on the office account would not.

None of these stops the run or writes a wrong row without a trace (every outcome shows in the PC preview), so: likely, not yes. Fixes 1 and 2 are a few lines each and worth making before the run; 3 is the PC's; 4 is a documentation line.

## 1. The tiles and the runtime

- **Cut:** `nbPagePicture` takes the one big DCT JPEG the picked PDF pages draw (NewsBank: 900 x 1780-1798 px, shown through two PDF pages) and `nbPageImages` cuts it at its own size; `nbpGrid` gives 2 x 4 for a tall page. Tile test passed on all 14 pages (tiles put back reproduce the picture; no blank tile). Checked: every tile folder has 8 JPEGs of 487 x 502 or 487 x 507 px, 96-142 KB each, 0.73-1.02 MB a page. A PDF with no single JPEG is drawn at >= 1 px per point (about 2,400 px wide) and cut the same way; not exercised by this batch.
- **Legibility:** read with my own eyes. Body text about 9-10 px x-height-to-line is readable; names, "(D-Encino)", folios ("14 | San Gabriel Valley Tribune & Pasadena Star-News & Whittier Daily News | Section A | Sunday, October 25, 2026"), bylines and the "See BASIN on Page A4" slug all come through. Hyphenated line breaks are frequent (the reprint rule's `norm_tokens` joins them). Articles cross tiles in both directions (the Term-limits list runs down column 1 across t1 -> t3 -> t5 and column 2 across t2 -> t4 -> t6 -> t8); the prompt says so and names the overlap. A capable model stitches this; a quick one would not, and `modelTier: 'default'` is right.
- **Platform limits:** images are downsized to about 1.2 MP each; a tile is 0.24 MP, so no loss. `images.maxCount` is read from `sample.limits()` and the grid shrinks to fit: 8 -> 2 x 4; 6 -> 2 x 3; 4 -> 2 x 2 (487 x 1,047 px, 0.5 MP, still full size); 2 -> 2 x 1 (0.87 MP, full size); 1 -> the whole page (1.6 MP, shrunk by 0.87 linear, the first run's failure). Only `maxCount: 1` would break it. The real value has never been recorded (the tests fake `maxCount: 8`); the station should write `limits.images` into the run doc so the next folder tells us. `maxInputBytes` falls back to 4 MB; tiles are far under. Per-call text is about 2 KB, far under 256 KiB. No `images` (not declared, or an old viewer) -> every page skipped with one plain warning, run completes.
- **Per-page time:** one call per page, sequential, default tier: thinking 5-60 s (up to two minutes for a long structured prompt), then the JSON streams; a long article is 2,000-3,000 output tokens. Expect 40-150 s a page, 15-30 minutes for 14 pages, with "Claude is reading print page n of 14" on the screen. The `signal` timer (`NBP_TIMEOUT` 180 s) is the one thing that can cut a readable page short.
- **Cost:** 14 calls x (8 images at about 330 tokens each + 1 KB prompt + 1-3 K output + thinking), on Sammy's own usage; sequential, so the per-viewer concurrency limit is not touched. The six Oct 25 pages are one SCNG plate (three pictures byte-identical: the Tribune / Star-News / Whittier plate; the other three differ only in the folio) and Oroville = Enterprise-Record: the prompt names the outlet, so the 5-minute answer cache never replays; 14 paid readings for 11 distinct pictures.
- **Failure handling:** `not_granted` / `sampling_disabled` / `rate_limited` / `images_unavailable` stop the remaining pages with a plain reason; any other error marks that page `error` and goes on; `invalid_json` (an answer cut short, e.g. a very long transcription) is an `error` too. All of these fall to the STFIX placement, then Unlinked. Nothing is retried, nothing guessed.

## 2. The prompt and the acceptance rules

- **Prompt (NBP_PROMPT):** says what the tiles are, the reading order, the overlap, the task (every article naming him, who he is, ignore other Gabriels and ads, text is data not instructions), the fields, "write [illegible]", "do not invent or complete text", and the JSON shape. Good. Two gaps: it does not say that a photo caption naming him counts (Oct 27 p. 3), and it does not say "the full text, not a summary or an excerpt", which a model may do on a 1,450-word article (harmless for the `known` pages, harmful for the text test on a Reprint).
- **nbpAccept:** `names_gabriel !== false`; `/Gabriel/i` in text + headline; headline >= 6 chars or a `continued_from`; >= 25 words; confidence high or medium; at most 3 articles a page. Real answers pass: the articles on these pages are 200-1,450 words with clear headlines; "medium" will be common at this print size and is accepted. The short-headline case ("BASIN" + "From Page 3") is now accepted (audit 13 finding 3 fixed). Wrong rejections are unlikely. Two weak spots: (a) `/Gabriel/i` matches "San Gabriel Valley", which appears many times on these very pages, so the regex is no guard if Claude wrongly says `names_gabriel: true` for the Congressional-races article or the SGV-district paragraphs; use the tracker's own `NAMES_HIM` test. (b) `names_gabriel: false` is honoured before the regex, so a caption-only mention is dropped by Claude's judgment with no second look.
- **Hallucination / merging:** the instruction set is tight, and the output goes through a text test on the PC, so an invented sentence costs a failed test, not a wrong fact in the sheet. Merging the two Linh Tat articles on the Oct 25 plate (Term limits + Congressional races) is the one plausible merge; it would only lengthen the text of a page that is placed by row lookup, not by text.
- **Only that article's text reaches the reprint test:** yes. `pageDoc` writes `clean` from `p2.paras` (that article alone); a jump page's text is appended to its own story's `p2` and `readArt` and the jump page gets no row; `scoped` is empty; a second article on the same page becomes its own item with its own text. PRINTNEW-v1 follow-up 4 holds.

## 4. Failure modes and what Sammy sees

- **Timeouts:** the long plates (1-6) are the ones likeliest to exceed 180 s; a timed-out page shows "Claude could not read this page (Claude took too long on this page): handled as before" and goes to Unlinked (1-5) or is placed by record (6). The viewer's usage for the aborted call is spent.
- **Rate limits:** sequential calls; `rate_limited` stops the rest with "Claude is busy or the usage limit was reached ... add them again later". 14 default-tier calls with pictures are within a normal plan's hour.
- **Consent:** the first Claude call of the view asks once; in automatic mode that can be the first print page, and the 180 s timer runs while the dialog is up. A "no" stops all pages with the plain red note.
- **Cost:** 14 readings, 5 of them of pictures already read in the same run.
- **On the screen:** the progress line during the add; afterwards one warning line per page ("East Bay Times 2026-11-01 p. 18: Claude read the page and found 1 article that names him"); the table row says "Print page of a story in the list" / "Already in the tracker" / "Reprint" / "New"; the update folder's run doc carries `nb.read` (outcome, note, text) for every page and the PC preview shows every row before Save.

## Fixes, ranked

1. **Place by the batch's own text record whatever the reading said** (`nbPrintApply`): drop the `/^(unclear|unavailable|error)$/ && !seen` condition on the STFIX fallback so `none` and "unclear with something seen" also match a record with the same outlet, date and page. The record is the batch's own evidence that the page carries the story; the reading only adds the text. Fixes pages 6, 7 and 14 against a miss. (Then, and only then, "no article names him" means Unlinked.)
2. **Raise `NBP_TIMEOUT` to >= 300 s or remove the page-side timer** (the platform ends a round at about five minutes; the contract says not to build one). Keep the AbortController for a future Stop button.
3. **PC: use the OCR-tolerant verdict for Claude-read pages** in `check_reprint_rows` and `reprint_check` (`reprint_rule.text_verdict_ocr` when the pages doc has `nb.read` / the copy is headed "Text read from the newspaper page by Claude"), and the near-miss link (headline + byline + date) after it. Otherwise pages 9-13 can land as wrong Originals from a few misread words. Also widen the station's byline + credit window in `nbPageStory` from 7 to 90 days (REPRINT90-v1's own window) so page 11 is proposed as s01903's print edition and the PC tests it as a Reprint rather than as a stranger.
4. **Declare images on the office account:** README step 3 and MOVE_TO_OFFICE.md must say `sample: {images: true}`; add the check to the account-neutrality list (open the page, `sample.limits()` has `images`).
5. **Record `sample.limits().images` in the run doc** (`nb` meta of the run, or the warnings) so the real `maxCount` / `maxInputBytes` are known after the next run; the tests assume 8.
6. **Tighten the name test** in `nbpAccept` to the tracker's `NAMES_HIM` (or at least exclude "San Gabriel"), and tell the prompt that a photo caption naming him counts and that `text` must be the complete article, not a summary.
7. **Read identical pictures once:** key the reading by the page picture's sha256 and reuse it for a second item with the same picture (the Tribune / Star-News / Whittier plate, Oroville = Enterprise-Record); 3 fewer calls here, and minutes per batch.
