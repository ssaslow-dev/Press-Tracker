# Audit: the "Add NewsBank PDFs" box on Sammy's real 12-story batch (multi3.pdf)

Independent, read-only audit (2026-11-03, 20:40-20:50 UTC). Judged from the PDF text (`pdftotext -layout`), the page images inside the PDF, the workbook copy `wb_0bccc1fc.xlsx`, both for-Update files (bc21c9fb and 0bccc1fc), the station code in the working tree, and a fresh run of the station page (Playwright, stubbed Claude/TinyFish, no network) against the 0bccc1fc file: drop, Add, Download. The run's outputs (items, table, run doc, zip) are in the scratchpad folder `audit12/out65/`. Note: `src/newsbank.js`, `src/nbtv.js`, `src/prjudge.js` and `src/station.js` were being edited while this audit ran (uncommitted NBTV-v1 work, 20:35-20:42 UTC); line numbers below are from the working tree at 20:42 and the page I ran was `dist/index.html` built 20:39.

## Verdict, in plain words

The reading of the PDF is right: 12 stories, the page spans, headlines, dates, outlets, sections, word counts, authors and NewsBank ids all match the PDF, and the nine "already in the tracker" calls are correct (each matched row holds the very same NewsBank record, not just a look-alike). The one "new" story (Bakersfield Californian, Dolores Huerta tributes) is genuinely new and its PDF in the update folder is exactly pages 31-37.

The results table still has real problems, and the two worst are not in what it says but in what it quietly drops:

1. **The two "print pages" are two real reprints, and they are lost.** The page images show that East Bay Times (Nov 1, page B6) and The Mercury News (Nov 3, page B3) both printed the Los Angeles Times / TNS story "California will add 'Non-Ultraprocessed Certified' seal to foods. What that means" (Karen Garcia), which quotes Gabriel on AB 2244 and is tracker story s01903 (Original 07053, LA Times, 2026-09-29). Neither paper has a row for it. The station turns them into two "Full Page ... to clip" lines in the run document with no image, no PDF and no story match, and nothing on the PC reads that list. Sammy's rules say the opposite: keep print originals (ORIGCOPIES-v1, PRINTORIG-v1) and record such pages (the workbook's "Unlinked print pages" tab, 1,443 rows, exists for exactly this).
2. **Print pages can never be recognised as already known.** The for-Update file carries no print-page ids at all (0 of 9,993 rows), because the "Unlinked print pages" tab is not exported, so the same page would be listed again on every run.

The rest are table and wording errors (the CNN outlet and date, press-release verdicts that come from a test stub, an unrecorded rule gap for known stories) and small things. Nothing in the batch would have written a wrong row to the workbook; the Bakersfield row is sound.

## Issues, ranked

### 1. The two print pages are real reprints of s01903 and are dropped on the floor (high)

**What is wrong.** Stories 8 and 9 are NewsBank "Full Page" image records: East Bay Times 2026-11-01 "Page: 18" (pages 25-27 of the batch; the image, 900x1798 JPEG, is on pages 26-27) and Mercury News, The 2026-11-03 "Page: 11" (pages 28-30; image on 29-30). NewsBank returned them for "Jesse Gabriel" because the page's OCR text names him. Reading the images: both pages carry the same article, headline "California will add 'Non-Ultraprocessed Certified' seal to foods. What that means", "By Karen Garcia, Los Angeles Times", printed folio B6 (Bay Area News Group, Section B, Sunday, November 1, 2026) and B3 (Section B, Tuesday, November 3, 2026), with the paragraph "'While Washington D.C. is paralyzed by inaction ...,' said Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel (D-Encino), author of Assembly Bill 2244." That is tracker story s01903 (Original 07053 Los Angeles Times 2026-09-29; print reprints already on the sheet: 05429 La Porte County Herald-Dispatch, 05430 Courier-Times, 05431 Times, 05437 Press Democrat, 00036 South Marion Citizen, all Edition Print / Date source "print edition"). No East Bay Times or Mercury News row exists for s01903 (Coverage search: no row of either outlet after 2026-10-20).

**What the station does.** `nbScreen` returns `print_page` (src/newsbank.js:50), no PDF is cut for it (src/newsbank_ui.js:103 cuts PDFs only for `new`/`reprint`), and `nbRun` only appends `{url, outlet, date, hl: 'Full Page', snippet}` to the run doc's `print_pages` (src/newsbank.js:221). The update zip I made holds nothing for them (3 files: manifest, run doc, one pages doc, one PDF for story 10). On the PC, `sheet_task.py` fills its own `print_pages` only from e-paper URL rows (`drop_skipped`, code/sheet_task.py:1386) and never reads a run doc's `print_pages`; the saver merely echoes that result key (work/saver/src/saver.py:47, 57). `sheet_task.py` reads the "Unlinked print pages" tab (PRINT_TAB, lines 544, 725-732) but has no writer for new rows on it. So the two records vanish after the keeper saves; the tracker's Update log would show "2 print pages to clip" and that is the end of it (NOQUEUE-v1 dropped the queue; clipping is paused, HANDOFF 3.13).

**Why this matters.** These are the only evidence that two Bay Area dailies ran the story in print; ORIGCOPIES-v1 says print-page PDFs are kept and attached once the match is confirmed, PRINTORIG-v1 names them `_original_pdf/<year>/<aid>_p<page>.pdf`, and the workbook's own convention for a NewsBank full-page record is an "Unlinked print pages" row (print-page link, APA citation (print page), Page, "Likely counterpart", "Clip priority": e.g. row 05432 Inland Valley Daily Bulletin 2026-10-04 p. 15, counterpart s00019).

**Fix.** (a) Cut the page PDF for a print page too (pages 25-27 / 28-30; same `nbStoryPdf`), carry it in the zip under the PRINTORIG-v1 name and record it in the pages doc. (b) Show the page image (pdf.js can render it) in the station list and ask the intern "which story is on this page?" with the headline/outlet/date typed or picked from the for-Update stories (ORIGCOPIES-v1: attach only after the intern confirms); with a confirmed match propose a Reprint row of that story (Edition Print, Page, Date source "print edition", NewsBank print-page link, APA citation (print page)); without one, propose an "Unlinked print pages" row. (c) On the PC, add the missing writer for "Unlinked print pages" rows and the `_original_pdf` placement. Until that exists, the station should at least say "2 print pages found: NOT carried into the update" instead of "listed to clip".

### 2. Print pages are never recognised as already in the tracker (high)

**What is wrong.** `nbScreen` checks `print_page` records only against pages seen in this session (`NBX.seen`, src/newsbank.js:50); `nbDocrefs()` (lines 19-25) indexes the for-Update file's links, and that file holds **zero** `image/` docrefs (checked: 0 of 9,993 rows in 0bccc1fc, likewise bc21c9fb), because `make_for_update.py` exports Coverage rows only (work/localapp/updatefolder/make_for_update.py:176: "every Coverage row incl. Reprints, Removed, unverified") and not the "Unlinked print pages" tab, whose 1,443 rows carry the print-page links. So a page already recorded there (or clipped) is listed as a fresh print page every time the batch, or any later batch holding it, is dropped.

**Fix.** Export the Unlinked tab's rows into the for-Update `rows` block (kind `P`, links = print-page link) and let `nbScreen` answer "already listed as a print page (05432)". `sheet_task.py` already reads that tab's IDs and links (PRINTTAB-v1), so the PC side is consistent.

### 3. Story 11 (CNN): the table's outlet and date note are wrong (medium)

**What is wrong.** The table says outlet "Story Is with Elex Michaelson, The [CNN]" and "(tracker dates it 09-09)" as if the tracker were odd. The tracker's spelling is **CNN** (row 00198 Outlet CNN; the show lives on the Audio & video tab: Show/program "The Story Is with Elex Michaelson", Air time "22:00 PT"; SHOWNET-v1: shows are not outlets). And the date is not a discrepancy but the rule: "Aired 1-2a ET" on Sept 10 is 10-11 pm PT on Sept 9 (REVIEW4-v1 F1, DATES-v1: broadcast = Pacific date; A&V row 00198 Air time 22:00 PT, Mention 1 at 01:23:28). The transcript is the whole hour (6,811 words); Gabriel's segment is pages 45-47 ([01:25:23]); 00198 is that segment with this same docref, so "known" is right.

**Cause.** `nbSpell` (src/newsbank.js:29-40) maps only to names the sheet already uses and finds no row spelled with the show name, so `outlet_clean` stays NewsBank's string; the list column shows `p.outlet_clean` (src/newsbank_ui.js:156). `tests/newsbank_real2_test.js:23, 42` asserts that string as "the tracker's spelling", so the test enshrines the error. The date in the list is `p.date` (NewsBank's ET date). The uncommitted NBTV-v1 code (src/nbtv.js; src/newsbank.js lines 42, 159, 169, 256-262) now computes medium TV, network CNN, show, date_pt 2026-09-09, air 22:00 PT for this item (I ran `NBTV.detect` on it: correct), but only for the proposed row; the list and the dedupe candidate still show the show name.

**Fix.** Show the tracker outlet (network) and the Pacific date in the list for transcripts; change the test's expectation for story 11 to outlet "CNN", date 2026-09-09 (source date 09-10 in the note).

### 4. ORIGCOPIES-v1 is not implemented for known stories, and the table hides it (medium)

**What is wrong.** Sammy: "I want all original copies saved even if they are already in the tracker" (ORIGCOPIES-v1): a NewsBank/ProQuest story matching a row is not dropped; its PDF is attached as an extra original copy and blank cells are filled. The station cuts no PDF and fills nothing for a `known` story (src/newsbank_ui.js:103; `nbRun` takes only `new`/`reprint`, src/newsbank.js:149); the table's "known ... No" reads as if that were the end of it. For THIS batch the loss is nil: all nine matched rows already hold the same NewsBank record as their saved copy (File name nb00038r-s00013.pdf, nb00049o-s00016.pdf, nb00031r-s04297.pdf, nb00173o-s00047.pdf, nb00131o-s00042.pdf, nb00165r-s00045.pdf, nb00133o-s00044.pdf, nb00198o-s00052.pdf, nb00082o-s00026.pdf). But several have blanks the record could fill under "fill blanks only" (00031: Topic, Tone, Role; 00165: Publisher, Role, Topic; 00049/00127: Outlet city), and the next batch may hold a NewsBank record for a web-only row, where the rule bites.

**Fix.** For `known`, cut the PDF when the matched row has no NewsBank PDF (or always, named per OTHERFILES-v1), carry a "fill" doc (row id, cells that are blank in the for-Update row and known from the record), and let the PC save append to "Other files". The table should say "known 00038: copy already in the library" or "known 00301: copy attached".

### 5. The press-release column is partly untested and inconsistently labelled (medium)

**What is wrong.** "Yes(text)" on story 1 and bare "Yes" on 3, 4, 5 describe the same basis: in my run all four are `yes / by: text` (1 and 3: "opens '... issued the following news release'" + release category tags; 4: "/PRNewswire/ --"; 5: "issued the following" + dateline). The "No" on 2, 6, 7, 11, 12 is not a judgment at all: the Playwright stub answers `press_release: 'no', pr_why: "a reporter's story"` for every item (tests/nb/real_harness.js stub in newsbank_real2_test.js:26; the run shows `by: Claude`), and no real Claude call has been made on this batch. The text signals alone give "none" for those five, which is the right answer, but the table presents a canned answer as a result. For the nine known items the verdict is moot (no row is written). The verdicts agree with the sheet (00038, 00031, 00173, 00131 are Press release = Yes), and under PRJUDGE-v2 they are "other issuer's release on a wire" (Central Coast Caucus, City of Goleta, Anheuser-Busch, the Governor), none his office's.

**Fix.** Label the column by basis ("yes: text signals", "no: no signals; Claude not run") and state that Claude was stubbed.
